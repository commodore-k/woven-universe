

A mysterious, advanced technique with [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md) that can, if done correctly, create the [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md)s. These are required for [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md) to exist in a Weave as the following are Composite Weave Aspects:
- Life
- Death
- Blood

To bond two or more Weave Cords together means the beings bound to the Weave Cords must undo parts of their Weave Cords and knit connections between their cords, thus entwining them together. However, bonding Weave Cords takes great power and immense focus from both beings involved in the Weave Cord bonding. This process of course also requires lifting up the Weave that the Weave Cords are made from.
