

A mysterious, advanced technique with [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md) that can, if done correctly, create the [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md)s required for [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md) to exist in a Weave.

To bond two or more Weave Cords together means the beings bound to the Weave Cords must undo parts of their Weave Cords and knit connections between their cords, thus entwining them together. However, bonding Weave Cords takes great power and immense focus from both beings involved in the Weave Cord bonding. This process of course also requires the Weave the Weave Cords are made from, to be "lifted".
