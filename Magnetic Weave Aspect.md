## Overview
This [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) was woven into the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md) during its [creation](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) by the [Allkin](Allkin.md).

The Allkin [Urda](Urda.md) later bonded herself to this Weave Aspect with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).

## Aspect

Imbues the ability to define and mutate the attraction/repulsion force of matter in any state in a Weave this Aspect is apart of (more or less the "magnetism" of matter).

## AllKin Weave Cord Use

- Can **permanently** ***or*** temporarily mutate the attraction/repulsion force of matter in the Terrum Weave. (regardless of Matter state).

## Terrum [Majik](Majik.md) Use

- Can temporarily attract or repulse matter from self (effect duration: while casting)
- Can temporarily modify the attraction or repulsion of matter (effect duration: while casting)
