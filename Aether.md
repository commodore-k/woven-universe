## Overview
The first and singular [Weave](Weave.md) of existence in [The Woven Universe](The%20Woven%20Universe.md). This is the Weave where all of the [Allkin](Allkin.md) reside. This Weave is an endless Void, the only things that existed at its creation are the 7 Allkin, nothing else.

The [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md)s that make up this Weave are unknown ,but it at minimum has enough defined to allow for the existence of the [Allkin](Allkin.md). We don't know what those Weave Aspects are to create such beings.
