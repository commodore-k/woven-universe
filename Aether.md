The first and singular [Weave](Weave.md) of existence in [The Woven Universe](The%20Woven%20Universe.md). This is the Weave where all of the [Allkin](Allkin.md) reside. It is a static, unchanging Weave.

The only [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md)s it has defined in its Weave are those that allow for the existence of the [Allkin](Allkin.md), nothing else exists but endless empty space.
