Kin Majik simply refers to Performing Majik by tapping into the powers of either
1. One of the 3 [Spliced Weave Cords](Weave%20Cord%20Splicing.md) that were created from the [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md) of the [Allkin](Allkin.md). These are also referred to as the [Allkin Bond](Allkin%20Bond.md)s.
2. One of the 3 [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md) OF the [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md)s that were CREATED by those 3 Spliced Weave Cord above. (i.e. [Dura](Dura.md)'s Weave Cords).
