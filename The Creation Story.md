### The Beginning of the Universe
Our Universe was born through some unknown means. At its birth, there was only one [Weave](Weave.md) of existence: the [Aether](Aether.md). And in this Weave resided beings of great power: The [Allkin](Allkin.md). The Allkin are made up of [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md) and [The All Fathers](The%20All%20Fathers.md). These being are powerful and immortal, but their Weave defined a static and unchanging world, save for the Allkin themselves. There is thus no life or death in the Aether Weave for the Allkin.

### The Weaving of [Terrum](Terrum.md)

The Allkin merely existed in their void-like weave, with nothing but their own existence to occupy their time. During this unknowable length of time, they slowly learned about the [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md) and charted out the [Woven Axioms](Woven%20Axioms.md).

Because of [The First Woven Axiom](The%20First%20Woven%20Axiom.md) of [The Woven Universe](The%20Woven%20Universe.md), the Allkin were prevented from being able to mutate their Weave to create or change things in it. They learned that they _could_ distort their own Weave, but in one like theirs where it was a void, it was near meaningless, save to quickly travel distances in the void. This was established as [The Second Woven Axiom](The%20Second%20Woven%20Axiom.md). However, after many years of further tinkering with the Warp & Weft, they discovered that they *could* create ***other*** weaves of existence AND those weaves ***could***  be mutated and changed. These findings are codified as [The Third Woven Axiom](The%20Third%20Woven%20Axiom.md) and [The Fourth Woven Axiom](The%20Fourth%20Woven%20Axiom.md). The catch with the latter two axioms is that it takes an immense amount of power to *properly* do this. When they were working on this, they figured with all 7 of the Allkin, they could manage the creation of a new Weave and be able to mutate it. If they were successful in this, they had a way to live for something more than drifting in this void of a Weave for eternity. They were of course successful, and those two axioms were established. They called the Weave they wove from their experiment [[Terrum]].

Because it took all 7 of the Allkin to reliably use the Warp and Weft to create or even interact with the Terrum Weave after its creation, the Allkin desired a way to more easily mutate and control the Terrum Weave without all having to congregate together and lift the Terrum Weave to mutate it in the way they wished. [Stel](Stel.md), one of [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md), had come up with the idea of [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md). The ability to create Weave Cords introduced [The Fifth Woven Axiom](The%20Fifth%20Woven%20Axiom.md).

And so, all 7 Allkin lifted up the *Terrum* Weave. They then took turns relinquishing their hold, with only six Allkin now holding up the *Terrum* Weave while the one worked on cording their Weave Cords.

When it was [Dura](Dura.md)'s (one of [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md)) turn to Cord her own Weave Cords, she saw that the others had already made multiple cords that together controlled all [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md)s of the *Terrum* Weave. Dura tried to make a Weave Cord for one of the Weave Aspects of Terrum even though there was already a Weave Cord for it. Dura found that she couldn't. It was then that the Allkin learn of [The Sixth Woven Axiom](The%20Sixth%20Woven%20Axiom.md). Once a Weave Cord is made for a given Weave Aspect, another cannot be made. Rastos (one of the [The All Fathers](The%20All%20Fathers.md), who was in a bit of a love triangle with Dura and Urda (another of [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md)) at the time tried to unbind one of his Weave Cords for Dura... but this is when they learned a horrible new truth. Once one binds a Woven Cord to themselves, the Cord can never be fully undone, nor can it ever be unbound. This came to be [The Seventh Woven Axiom](The%20Seventh%20Woven%20Axiom.md). In Rastos's attempt to undo his cord, he did discover one more Axiom. While the Seventh Woven Axiom prevents you from undoing a cord completely, one can still modify their cord slightly. Nothing that would ultimately change what Weave Aspect it controlled, but enough that it left open some room for imagination. This was [The Eight Woven Axiom](The%20Eight%20Woven%20Axiom.md).

With nothing left to do, they all set down the *Terrum* Weave, leaving Dura as the only Allkin with no Weave Cords.

## The Bonding Of The Allkin

After untold amounts of time that the Allkin spent time learning to use their Weave Cords to mold the *Terrum* Weave into something interesting: making stars, planets, and everything in-between in the *Terrum* Weave, they still felt dissatisfied. At the end of the day, they were merely on lookers into this Weave, but they couldn't *experience* it. 

During this time, the Allkin, including Rastos, had grown apart from Dura. They were lost in their uses of their Weave Cords and imagining what *Terrum* could become. Rastos and Urda, both working on the Terrum project grew much closer together. Meanwhile, Dura was left to wander the void of their Weave with nothing to do, and now this time, she didn't even have other Allkin to converse with.

The 6 Allkin, reviewing their learned [Woven Axioms](Woven%20Axioms.md) thought of something. They exist on a Weave, one they call *Aether*. Because of [The First Woven Axiom](The%20First%20Woven%20Axiom.md), they cannot mutate it. However, Aether is a Weave, and thus it has its own set of Weave Aspects. Their Weave is more or less a void, BUT they the Allkin exist in it, and they are living beings with immense power that have now successfully created and can mutate another weave thanks to [The Fourth Woven Axiom](The%20Fourth%20Woven%20Axiom.md). Thus, there must be some set of Weave Aspects in their weave that allow sentient beings like themselves to exist in a Weave. In the *Terrum* Weave they created, there is no life, the closest they could get was the plant life that Rastos had created, but it could not become sentient like them. They didn't know what Weave Aspects contributed to their existence as living beings in the Aether Weave. However, if they could figure out what they thought were the Weave Aspects that allowed for sentient beings and replicate that in the *Terrum* Weave, then they could use those beings as puppets to then mutate their own weave. Like a puppet master controlling a puppet to control the puppet master's own world. And the [Woven Axioms](Woven%20Axioms.md) allowed this, nothing in what they'd discovered technically prevented this. They could change their Weave into something beautiful!

As always, it was the All Mother [Stel](Stel.md) that came up with the potential solution. Through her studies of the Weave, she formulated the idea of [Weave Cord Bonding](Weave%20Cord%20Bonding.md) to make [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md)s. Her hope was that with this, they could create what she called [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md). A form of life that she believed could reach sentience like them. If they also made Weave Cords *TO* the Composite Weave Aspects they made, then they _might_ be able to control these beings. This would mean they could essentially control beings of a different weave who could in-turn mutate their own weave, there by allowing them, by proxy, to create worlds in their Aether Weave that they could then directly experience for the first time, and [The Fourth Woven Axiom](The%20Fourth%20Woven%20Axiom.md) allows this.

If the Allkin were going to make these Weave Cord Bonds, they would need all 7 of the Allkin to lift up the *Terrum* Weave. This meant they needed Dura... however, they were aware that they had done nothing but ignore their sister for ages, leaving her to drift alone in their Weave. When they finally found her, they tried to apologize, and they asked if she would help. She knew their apologies were hollow, but they told her of their plan, and that with it, they she could no longer be alone. She initially refused... she had already gone through the stages of grief. But then a plan hatched in her mind. It would be the hardest thing she'd ever done, but she felt that this was the only opportunity for her, there may never be a next time where the *Terrum* Weave is lifted and she could make Weave Cords of her own, since with this project they would be introducing these new Composite Weave Aspects. She changed her answer and told them that she would help.

And so, all 7 Allkin used their combined powers to lift the *Terrum* Weave, and as each pair took turns relinquishing their hold on the *Terrum* Weave to bond, the other 5 held it up under immense strain so the other two could work on bonding their weave cords. During this long arduous process, Dura secretly and quietly corded her own Cords of power. Trying to do so was immensely difficult for her and pushed her to her absolute limits but she persisted, determined to have her own cords. As the Allkins created each new Composite Weave Aspect with their Weave Cord Bond, she would work as quickly as possible to make a Weave Cord for that Composite Weave Aspect. When she made the Weave Cord for the Blood Composite Weave Aspect, she mess it up a bit as she was trying to do this while helping hold up the Terrum Weave. The Weave Cord was intact but part of it was frayed. The bonding was wrapping up so she didn't have time to fix the fraying of the Blood Weave Cord.

When the bonding process was completed, they moved onto the last step of the process: to create new Weave Cords to have control over the life, death, and blood Composite Weave Aspects that created the [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md), their children. But it was then that they found that the Weave Cords for these Composite Weave Apsects already existed and saw they were bound to Dura. They relinquished their hold on the Terrum Weave, furious with Dura, but knowing about [The Seventh Woven Axiom](The%20Seventh%20Woven%20Axiom.md) they new the bonds were final and that nothing could be done. The Allkins pleaded with her to do her bidding for them. Dura, having been left in silence for so long had come to accept that life and that, though she didn't create the [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md), she now had domain over them with her Weave Cords. She decided she would instead watch over them, and let them grow in their own way and lead their own life, regardless of whether they ever evolved into sentience. With that, Dura used the Warp & Weft to distort her position in the Aether Weave to move herself vast distances from her brotherkin and sisterkin. The 6 Bonded Allkins despised her. They called her greedy, and narrow minded. From then on, they used what powers they did have over Terrum with their Weave Cords to try and influence the course of evolution as best they could. Their original plan would take much much longer.

## The Whispering Weavers And The Blood Addiction

The Bonded Allkin were all too excited when they finally worked evolution towards the first sentient creatures: Humans. [Cerus](Cerus.md) used his Corded power of the Sky and Wind to whisper their stories to their sentient children. But it was ultimately up to the humans to not only hear these stories created on the winds, but to believe in them. Importantly, he left out the existence of Dura in his stories, as they hated her and didn't want them knowing of her and what she did for them.

Cerus told their children only part of how the [Blood Weave Connection](Blood%20Weave%20Connection.md) works and only showed them how to make anchors, configure them to touch their Weave Cord Bonds, and bind the anchors to themselves. He called the usage of their powers [Majik](Majik.md). Terrum humans took to calling it Blood-Majik, or more specifically Kin-Majik.

When the first human pulled on the power of the [Cerel](Cerel.md) [Allkin Bond](Allkin%20Bond.md) with their rudimentary anchor, Cerus and Stel were surprised to find that some residual blood had somehow leaked into their own Weave. The Allkin didn't know what to do with this bit of Terrum blood, but they wondered at what it could be used for. At some point, Cerus decided to taste the blood, curious what it tasted like. The blood acted as a euphoric drug, it let Cerus experience things he never had before, it was essentially a hallucinogenic for them. The Aether Weave caused Terrum blood to change properties when it existed in their Weave. It was then that the Allkin developed a thirst for the blood of their created children. As their addiction waxed, their plan for using the humans to recreate their own Weave waned. They became ever more ensconced in extracting this blood from the humans.

Dura was the exception. She watched in horror from afar as the Allkin became obsessed with the different usages of Terrum blood somehow existing in their own Weave. She saw that they never cared for their blood-life creations. They were after all only ever regarded as tools to them, but now that view had been perverted further from the taste of their blood.

While Dura wasn't technically their creation mother, she did still hold domain over the created blood-life.

She wept for humans most as out of all the blood-life in the *Terrum* Weave that she loved unconditionally, the Allkin were abusing them the most as they could be manipulated to sacrifice their blood to them. She also wept for her adopted children did not know of her existence, she wept because she could not communicate with them, and because she could not stop the other Allkin. They were all immortal, fighting each other would be useless, and the domain she had control over gave her no way to communicate to the living. Or at least none that she knew.

Dura did the only thing she could think of, and that was finding Cerus in the void of the Aether Weave. When she did, she waited for him to Whisper on the wind to the humans as he told them one of their made up stories. When he did this, she snuck up behind Cerus while he was whispering to the humans and behind him, she whispered her story, hoping that she was quiet enough for Cerus to not hear, but loud enough for her voice to travel along with Cerus's, hoping someone in the *Terrum* Weave would hear her story on the winds. When Cerus stopped whispering, he heard someone else behind him whispering. Enraged with Dura not only for coming back, but sneaking up and attempting to talk to his created children was beyond the pale in his eyes. He informed the others and then they this time used the Warp & Weft to move far away from her in the void, where they then proceeded to form the blood they'd been collecting into solid objects and build a blood palace that would sequester them away from Dura so they could whisper lies laced into truths to the humans un-interrupted. Dura had domain over Terrum Blood yes, but she could only control in when it was in the Terrum Weave, as her power/domain over it is made from a Terrum Weave Cord. So when residual blood seeped into the Aether Weave, it was now defined by the Weave Aspects of the Aether Weave, so she had no more control over it than the others did.

Meanwhile, in Terrum, there was one woman who heard Dura's whispers on the wind. Who this woman was or when she lived was lost to time. But she spread the story of the 4th Mother, of Dura. Many considered these stories blasphemous as there were only ever 3 All Mothers and 3 All Fathers and that they had proof as there were no anchor configurations apart from the ones taught to them by the whispers of the Allkin. But some believed. These believers were the [Durians](Durians.md).

One day, Dura noticed blood had started seeping on one of her Weave Cords. She wept for a long while at the sight of this as it meant humans of Terrum had figured out how to use majik with her Weave Cords. Seeing the craving in here siblings, she refused to drink the blood like they did. She thought back to the Rastos she used to know and love. She missed that man. In memory of the man he used to be, the man she used to love, and for her adopted blood-life children, she used the blood to form a blood garden. She called it the Passing Gardens. In this void, blood was the only object that existed that they could mold as they saw fit. She could only replicate the *shapes* of the plants that Rastos had once shown here from Terrum. Since the garden was made entirely of blood, none of these were actual plants, and it was comprised of different shades of red and made to look as close to the plants as she could remember. She could differentiate blood so she new which blood was from the same person. She used this knowledge to create plants from a single person's blood.  She tried to make it as pleasant looking as she could and wanted the plants to act as a monument to each of her children even though no one but her would ever experience it.

## Dura's Frayed Blood Weave Cord

Dura has domain over blood, the whole of it, but the solid components of blood tend to be more attracted to Dura and the other Allkin, so when blood of a Majus run along any of their Weave Cord Bonds or Dura's Weave Cords, the solid parts drag the rest of the blood along the cord towards them, this makes even using Dura's Frayed Blood Weave Cord generally safe. However, if humans were to figure this out and use only the liquid part of their blood: plasma, with Dura's Frayed Blood Weave Cord, then they would find that what they tap into is not anything they would've ever expected. Plasma is still part of blood so it can still run along her Weave Cords by itself, but without the attractive force of the solid parts of blood, the plasma always seems to course up the Blood Weave Cord and out of the frayed part, touching the Warp and Weft itself. Doing this allows a Majus to use the Warp and the Weft directly, the few who have learned this call it WeaveMajik, the other branch of Bloodmajik. A single human can become a Weave Majus and control the Warp and the Weft of their own Weave without needing immense power, though it is harder to wield and is more draining since plasma is the larger makeup of blood. But if one were to try and control a Weave external to their own, it would surely kill them, they would need lots of plasma equal to many humans, and even then, who knows what the results would be from such unskilled Weave Majii.
