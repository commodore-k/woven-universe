## The Wind Whispers of Allkin

Cerus spun a story of a Pantheon of all powerful gods:

Hello my children, I am Cerus, the Allkin of Sky, Wind, and Storms. The Allkin are your creators, and you are our children, you have 3 All Mothers: Stel - the All Mother of Stars, Light, and Astral movements; Urda - The Mother of Terris and all planets; and [Arda](Arda.md): - The All Mother of Fire and Heat; you have 3 All Fathers 
The Allkin, 3 All Mothers, and 3 All Fathers, each destined for one another. They existed in a Weave that was a beautiful evergreen garden that, like them, was immortal. But also like them, it was unchanging. No plants grew, none died. Nothing ever blossomed for everything was already in blossom and bloom. The Allkin wanted to see change, to see life flourish. And finally, but most tragically of all, they could not fix their own Weave, they could not instill change in it, [The Fifth Woven Axiom](The%20Fifth%20Woven%20Axiom.md) forebade them. However, with [The Third Woven Axiom](The%20Third%20Woven%20Axiom.md) and their great powers, they wielded the Warp & Weft to create [Terrum](Terrum.md) a new Weave for the children they would bear. They wanted better for their children, so they wove in Weave Aspects that allowed for change, for life, for death. Each All Mother and All Father created a Set of Weave Cords for themselves that granted them domain of a set of "Majik" forces in [Terrum](Terrum.md). They then used these "Majiks" to mold this Weave into a living home for their children. This was a dangerous process which is why they couldn't yet give brith to blood-life. Finally, once all was in place, the All Mothers and All Fathers came together to bond with their destined mate, in this process to show their eternal devotion, The All Mother and All Father of each bond performed a Splicing of all their weave Cords, for once spliced, it cannot be undone. And in this bonding process, they were able to create blood-life, and from there, they guided blood-life to create their "Verakin": the true children of the Allkin. Verakin can use their blood to touch the Cords of their Allkin granting them access to the Majik in them. If you are our Verakin, then we will teach you how to wield these Majiks.

But before we teach you, in order to know if you are our Verakin that we seek, you must show us that you can perform 3 tasks that each represents one of the 3 Allkin Bonds that made you. If you are our indeed our Verakin, then you will perform these tasks without question, knowing that you can trust our wind-words.

When the Allkin saw the humans eventually listening, they proceeded with the next part.

### The First Task - Charting The Allkin

The first task you must perform to prove you are the Verakin of the Allkin, is to accurately chart all of the stars in my night sky, and use the days of Terris with your star charts to determine how long your Terris year is. This will show your devotion to your All Mother Stel how made your sun and night sky, and to Me your All Father the one would provided you with wind in your lungs and a Sky to see your mothers work.

The Allkin figured this was a good aptitude test as well as a test of obedience.

### The Second Task - The Rastur Mountain

Cerus whispered the first task for humans to prove that they were the true children of the Allkin:

"The First task you must perform to prove you are the Verakin of the Allkin, is to plant a Blackthorn bush on the highest peak of the tallest mountain. This will show your devotion to your All Mother Urda and your All Father Rastos.

### The Third Task - Marda

The Third and final task you must perform to prove you are the Verakin of the Allkin, you must build a large ship and hunt down the sea beast you know as the Kraken. Bring the corpse of the beast to land and Burn it in a grand pyre. This will show you devotion to your All Mother Arda, and your All Father Marus.


The completion of all 3 tasks took many years, but it also pushed the humans to develop as a people, creating new technology and skills to complete the tasks: metal working, glass making, ship building. The Allkin were impressed and happy that they were persistent through all those years, and so they taught them how to use [Majik](Majik.md).
