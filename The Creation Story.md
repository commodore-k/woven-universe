### The Beginning of the Universe
Our Universe was born through some unknown means. At its birth, there was only one [Weave](Weave.md) of existence: [Aether](Aether.md). And in this Weave resided beings of great power: The [Allkin](Allkin.md). These being are powerful and immortal, and their plane is static, and unchanging. There is no life or death in the Aether.

### The Weaving of [Terrum](Terrum.md)

The Allkin, so the story goes, were bored, faced with an eternity of nothing happening and no way of dying, they grew to feel like they were in a cage, trapped by their weave, and for seemingly no reason.

So all 7 Allkin came together to wield the Warp and the Weft: the two fundamental forces of the Universe, to weave a new Weave of existence, one where they could act as gods. They called this Weave of existence *Terrum*.

While controlling the Weave powers within ones own Weave is much more manageable, one using it to create another weave, or to mess with a Weave external to your own, is extremely difficult. But together, the 7 Allkin were able to build this stable Weave external to their own. 

Because it took all 7 of the Allkin to reliably use the Warp and Weft to create and interact with the Terrum Weave, the Allkin needed a way to sustainably interact with this weave without the need for all of them. Stel, one of the All Mothers, had come up with the idea of Weave Cords.

A Weave Cord is a cording of the Warp and Weft of a Weave that allows one external to the Weave to control a single aspect of said Weave. It however must also be bound to the user. You could think of a Weave Cord as a single string on a puppet. But to create a Weave Cord requires the Weave to be lifted. Lorekeepers aren't quite sure what Cerus meant by this, but Iin any case, when a weave is held up, one can create a Weave Cord.

And so, all 7 Allkin lifted up the Terrum Weave. They then took turns relinquishing their hold, with only six Allkin now holding up the Terrum Weave while one worked on cording their Weave Cords.

When it was Dura's turn to Cord her own Weave Cord powers, she saw that the others had already made multiple cords that together controlled all aspects of Terrum. And shortly after, the other 6 had relinquished their holding of the Terrum Weave, not bothering to give her the time to even cord her own powers.

## The Bonding Of The Allkin

After untold amounts of time that the Allkin took to making stars, planets, and everything in-between in the Terrum Weave, they still felt like not much was happening. They decided they wanted to re-create themselves in Terrum. And so, Stel being the wisest of the Allkin, also formulated a plan for how they may create life in Terrum: Weave Cord Bonding.

For two Allkin to bond, this means they must undo parts of their cords of power and weave connections between their cords, entwining them together. Doing this would allow them to create life. However, doing this also takes great power and immense focus from both Allkin bonding, and it of course also requires the Weave to be held up. It isn't clear to the lorekeepers exactly how Weave Cord Bonding created life, only the Allkin can truly comprehend this.

There were however only 7 Allkin who knew of each other, and [Dura](Dura.md) as we know was, for some reason unknown to us lorekeepers, the least liked. She was also the only Allkin without any Cords to the Terrum Weave at the time. The Allkin needed all 7 to help in the process while 2 Allkin bonded, with the rest needed to hold up the Terrum Weave. When the Allkin came to Dura, she initially refused... but then a plan hatched in her mind. It would be the hardest thing she'd ever done, but she felt it was the only opportunity that would allow her to create her own Cords of power, and maybe even to get back at the others, she didn't know if there'd ever be a next time the Terrum Weave would be lifted, it would be her only opportunity at creating Weave Cords. She accepted to help.

And so, as each pair took turns relinquishing their hold on Terrum to bond, the others held it up so the two could work on bonding their weave cords. During this long arduous process, Dura secretly and quietly corded her own Cords of power. Trying to do so was immensely difficult for her. But the Allkin were creating something new in Terrum during this bonding process: living creatures. The bonding process created them, but it didn't give domain or power of them. To do this, one would need to create new, separate Weave Cords. And so, Dura while helping to hold up the Terrum Weave, she also made cords of power that gave her domain over the life, death, blood, and the souls of the living creatures that the others were actively creating in Terrum. It was extremely difficult to hold AND cord, and her Weave Cord for the power of Blood was frayed and she ran out of time to fix it.

When the bonding process was completed, they moved onto the last step of the process to create new Weave Cords to have control over life, death, blood, and soul of their children. But they found that the cords for these aspects of the Weave already existed and saw they were bound to Dura. They relinquished their hold on the Terrum Weave, furious with Dura, but knowing the ceremony was over and nothing more could be done. The Allkin cast her out, and never wanted to see her or hear from her again. She had stolen domain over the children THEY created. They thought her evil, and greedy.

## The Whispering Weavers And The Blood Addiction

The Bonded Allkin were all too excited when they finally worked evolution towards the first sentient creatures: Humans. They wouldn't last long in the grand scheme of things but the milestone for them was a win. [Cerus](Cerus.md) used his Corded power of the Sky and Wind to whisper their stories to their sentient children. But it was ultimately up to the humans to not only hear these stories created on the winds, but to believe in them. Importantly, he left out the existence of Dura in his stories.

They also told their children of the blood connection they have to them, and that if they ever needed to use a small amount of the powers of their Bonded Cords to help survive in Terrum, then all they had to do was sacrifice a bit of blood as it would traverse their Bonded Cords of Power that made living creatures, and effectively allow their children to momentarily tap into the powers of those bonded Cords. Cerus called this pulling of the power Majik. Humans later called it BloodMajik. And so, Cerus taught humans how to make an anchor and bind it to them so that they could use this Majik. Unknown at the time to humans was that they could do the same with Dura's cords of power, however, her cords were unbonded and did not make living creatures. BUT, her Cords did hold power over blood itself, and so this alone was what allowed the children of the Allkin to still use blood to tap into Dura's Cords of Power.

When the first human pulled on the power of the Cerel bond with their rudimentary anchor, Cerus and Stel were surprised to find that some residual blood had somehow leaked into their own Weave. The Allkin didn't know what to do with this bit of Terrum blood, but they wondered at what it could be used for. At some point, Cerus decided to taste the blood, curious what it tasted like. The blood acted as a euphoric drug, it let Cerus experience things he never had before. It was then that the Allkin developed a thirst for the blood of their children. They were sure that they would *eventually* continue with their original goal, but for now, they obsessed over extracting the blood of these creatures.

All save Dura. She watched in horror from afar as the Allkin became ensconced with the different usages of Terrum blood somehow existing in their own Weave. She saw that they never cared for their creations, only thinking about the end goal. And now, humans were like cattle, a new side project of the Allkin. Dura wasn't technically their mother, but she wept for humans the most as out of all the living creatures that she loved unconditionally, the Allkin were abusing them the most as they could be manipulated to sacrifice their blood to them. She also wept for her adopted children did not know of her existence, she wept because she could not communicate with them, and because she could not stop the other Allkin. All she could do is ease their suffering when their Terrum form perished. Dura's Cords of power are the only ones where Terrum souls, in addition to Terrum blood, can run along her Weave Cords from the Terrum Weave to the Aether Weave. Dura created the Passing Gardens as a safe place for Terrum souls to recover from their life. When they are ready, Dura sends their Terrum souls back to Terrum along her Soul Weave Cord to become one with the Terrum Weave, sending them into blissful oblivion.

Dura did try once to sneak up behind Cerus while he was whispering to the humans and behind him, she whispered her story, hoping that she was quiet enough for Cerus to not hear, but loud enough for her voice to travel along with Cerus's, hoping someone in terrum would hear her story on the winds. Cerus stopped whispering only to hear someone else behind him whispering. Enraged with Dura not only for coming back, but sneaking up and attempting to talk to his children was beyond the pale in his eyes. They erected their own palace and made sure it was one that Dura could not enter so that they may interact with their subjects un-interrupted. Meanwhile, in Terrum, there was one woman who heard Dura's whispers on the wind. Who this woman was or when she lived was lost to time. But she spread the story of the 4th Mother, of Dura. Many considered these stories blasphemous. But some believed. These believers were the [Durians](Durians.md).


## Dura's Cords

Dura has domain over blood, the whole of it, but the solid components of blood tend to be more attracted to Dura and the other Allkin, so when blood of a Maji run along any of her Weave cords, the solid parts drag the rest of the blood along the cord towards her, this makes even using her blood Weave Core (which is frayed) generally safe. However, if humans were to figure this out and use only the liquid part of their blood: plasma, along Dura's Blood Weave Cord, then they would find that what they tap into is not anything they would've ever expected. Plasma is still part of blood so it can still run along her Weave Cords, but without the attractive force of the solid parts of blood, the plasma always seems to course up the cord and out of the frayed part, touching the Warp and Weft itself. Doing this allows a Majus to use the Warp and the Weft directly, the few who have learned this call it WeaveMajik. A single human can become a Weave Majus and control the Warp and the Weft of their own weave without needing immense power, though it is harder to wield and is more draining since plasma is the larger makeup of blood. But if one were to try and control a Weave external to their own, it would surely kill them, they would need lots of plasma, and even then, who knows what the results would be.
