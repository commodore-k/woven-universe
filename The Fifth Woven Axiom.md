This is the Fifth Axiom of the [Woven Axioms](Woven%20Axioms.md) that govern Weaving with the [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md).

A being of a given weave may use the Warp and Weft of another Weave to make a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md). Bonding a Weave cord gives the being the ability to reliably mutate/control a single aspect of that Weave with considerably less power needed from the being.
