This is the Fifth Axiom of the [Woven Axioms](Woven%20Axioms.md) that govern Weaving with the [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md).

[Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md) can be used to bind a being in one [[Weave]] to a single [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) of a different Weave. Multiple Weave Aspects of a Weave may be bound to a single being of another Weave.
