## Founding

The Emberal Empire was established around 2431 [3E](Third%20Era.md). It was established by a small family of the name: Kordona. They were at the center of a rebellion in the Ancient Kinnish Empire. It was rotting from the inside, the Kinnish were abusing their subjects and growing lazy with the protection of their lands. The Rebellion ultimately didn't have to do much to push over this Empire which was eponymously named after the Patriarchal bloodline that ruled it. From the ashes of this one, a new empire was established by the Kordona bloodline, the Emberal Empire.


A Matriarchy with a primary Matriach and an advisory Matriarch

## Matriarch Ascension System

The Emberal Empire wanted to ensure that fresh young blood was always at the fore front of the Empire to instill strength and youthfulness. It also ensured that no single Matriarch had her hands on power for too long. So the two court system was established:

### Solar Court

This court is headed by a single Matris, she is the Queen and sole Matriarch of the Emberal Empire. She is accompanied by her retinue of staff, from the Imperial treasury, Commander of Arms, etc. She has final say on all Imperial dealings throughout her Imperium. This queen is typically referred to by the following:

- The Matriarch
- The Sol Matris
- The Solar Matris
- The Queen
- The Solar Mother

The Eldest daughter of the Current Solar Matris succeeds her mother when she turns 30.

A Solar Matris is expected to Bond at least 3 times with 3 different men. Each must be dedicated to each of the 3 [Allkin Bonds](Allkin%20Bond.md). She is expected to produce at least one daughter. After these 3 bonds she is free to bond with whomever she wishes, Man or woman, and is free to dedicate or not dedicate those bonds, (though not dedicating is usually frowned upon). While not technically illegal, it would cause an uproar if she were to dedicate any of her bonds to [Dura](Dura.md).

When she ascends, she will bear the Sol crown, a crown with sun ray like spikes sticking out, made in a rare metal that has a [Magescent](Magescent.md) color to it.
### Lunar Court

This court is comprised of all the past living Solar Matrii. When they descend from the Solar Court for their respective daughter to succeed them, they then ascend to the Lunar court. Here they play on an advisory role to the Solar Matris. Combined, they also act as a stand-in temporary Solar Matris for internal issues when the Solar Matris is away dealing with external issues at far away courts.

Matris of this Court may be referred to as:
- Lunar Matris
- Evening Mother
- Evening Mothers
