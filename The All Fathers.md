The Three All Fathers
- [Marus](Marus.md)
- [Cerus](Cerus.md)
- [Rastos](Rastos.md)



## Terrum Understanding
The 3 All fathers carry the spirit, vitality, and the seed of life. Without them, there would be no trees (meaning all plantlife), sky, nor water. These are the domains of the 3 All Fathers.

## Reality
The 3 All Fathers are immensely powerful beings, equally as powerful as [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md) and they too exist in the [Aether Weave](Aether.md) in [The Woven Universe](The%20Woven%20Universe.md).
