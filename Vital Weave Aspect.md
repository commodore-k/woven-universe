## Overview
This [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) was woven into the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md) during its [creation](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) by the [Allkin](Allkin.md).

The Allkin [Rastos](Rastos.md) later bonded himself to this Weave Aspect with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).

## Aspect

Imbues the ability for any type of life to have some level of vitality which can modify its heartiness/constitution in a Weave this Aspect is apart of. Useless if Weave has no Weave Aspect(s) that allow for any type of life.

## Allkin Weave Cord Use

- Can create/destroy definitions of types of Vitality for life in the Terrum Weave
- Can control the vitality of existing life in the Terrum Weave

## Terrum [Majik](Majik.md) Use

- Can control the vitality of existing life in the Terrum Weave
