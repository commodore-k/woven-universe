In this world the "Gods" are referred to instead as "Allkin". They are the family that, by their own powers built the universe, with various aspects falling under their respective domain. But only together could they create living creatures. And so, when the Allkin bonded, they brought life into the universe. They cannot directly interact with the world as we do, but using the forces under their domain, they can indirectly sculpt and guide how the world changes, influence how their creations evolve. Many believe we humans are the culmination of their work over untold amounts of time, others believe we are but a stepping stone as they work to create creatures that reflect themselves in the physical world more closely. The latter would be right, but they'll never know.

The two parts of this pantheon are [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md) and the [The All Fathers](The%20All%20Fathers.md). When the bonded, the formed [Allkin Bonds](Allkin%20Bond.md).
