## Overview

(See [The Creation Story](The%20Creation%20Story.md) for more historical details)

The Allkin are powerful, immortal being that reside in the [Aether](Aether.md) Weave. This Pantheon of Allkin is comprised of two groups, [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md) and [The All Fathers](The%20All%20Fathers.md). They are real entities that mettle with [Terrum](Terrum.md). They each hold domain (power) over different aspects of the Terrum weave. They cannot directly interact with the Terrum Weave as we, being OF terrum do, but using the forces under their domain, they can indirectly sculpt and guide how the the Terrum Weave changes. Without one or the other, living creatures could not exist in Terum, it takes both the All Mothers AND the All Fathers to create living creatures. For an unknowable amount of time however, they instead played with their powers building out the Terrum Weave as we understand today. It wasn't until the forming of the [Allkin Bonds](Allkin%20Bond.md) that birthed living creatures into the world. It was at this point that they began acting as sculptors, using their powers as indirect scalpels, nudging evolution after its initial birth this way and that. Many believe we humans are the culmination of their work over untold amounts of time. Others believe we are but a stepping stone as they work to create creatures that reflect themselves in the Terrum Weave more closely. The latter would be right, but they'll never know as they are but mortal.

## Pantheon

[Rastos](Rastos.md): The Father of the Garden, bonded with [Urda](Urda.md), the Mother of Earth
- This Allkin bond is often referred to as "Rastur" / "The Rastur Bond"
- Represents peace, joviality, comedy, growth, long life, and happiness

[Cerus](Cerus.md), The Father of the Sky, bonded with [Stel](Stel.md), The Mother of Stars
- This Allkin bond is often referred o as "Cerel" / "The Cerel Bond"
- Represents love, time, intelligence, logic

[Marus](Marus.md), The Father of Seas, bonded with [Arda](Arda.md), The Mother of Fire
- This Allkin bond is often referred to as "Marda" / "The Marda Bond"
- Represents power, strength, emotion, sailing

[Dura](Dura.md): The Mother of Death, bonded with no-one, and is largely believed to not exist, and is severely looked down upon and socially not accepted to worship (though no laws technically bar it at least in the Emberal Empire, the same cannot be said for other Empires).

Whenever two people are bonded (marriage in Terrum), they usually dedicate their bonding to one of the Allkin Bonds. It is considered a bad omen to dedicate your marriage to a single AllKin as it may make the other of the Bond jealous and bring bad luck to the marriage.

None dedicate their bonding to Dura, the Mother of Death, and if they do, none make it known. Since most don't believe in her, they think it is foolish and also chalk it up to a bad omen, but [Durians](Durians.md) claim that it wouldn't as she is not in a bond and so there is no other Allkin to become jealous. However, most ignore that, but speaking as a lorekeeper, I can say the Durians are on to something there. But what pray tell would her martial blessings bring I wonder? For now, not even us lorekeepers know.

## Notes
Any family name using some combination of an Allkin bond  names usually has familial history of rituals focused on that Allkin bond. For example, the [Rastuu Brothers](The%20Rastuu%20Brothers.md) are part of the `Rastuu` family. This name was inspired by the Allkin bond of Rastur: Rastos & Urda.

Marus & Arda as a pair are often used to refer to things that are turbulent or to things that don't mix well, are conflicting but paired. (relating to the pair controlling water and fire)

In this world, the term marriage isn't a thing, they use bonding, since it is the same term they use to describe the pairings of their gods. And so, they imitate their gods through their bonding ceremonies (i.e. a wedding) and dedicate it to one of the Allkin bonds.
