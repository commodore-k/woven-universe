## Overview

[Kin-Majik](Kin-Majik.md) & [Weave-Majik](Weave-Majik.md) are both "BloodMajik". BloodMajik is the ability for [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md) to use their blood to "touch" / "pull" on the [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md)s.


Very few in the *Terrum* Weave even know of Weave-Majik, While the sect of Durians that discovered it are unaware of it, it is actually an un-intended consequence of [Dura](Dura.md)'s Blood Weave Cord being frayed.


There is no other type of majik system in Terrum. If you wish to use the powers of [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md) or Bonded Weave Cords, or the [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md) itself (thanks to Dura's Frayed Weave Cord), then blood, in some way, MUST be sacrificed.



## Majik Anchors & Binding

Power of a Weave Cord === BloodMajik

An Anchor allows a Bloodlife to touch a set of similar Weave Aspects. These sets of similar Weave Aspects are called "Schools of Majik". For an anchor to "anchor" to school of magic, it must have a specific structure. And then in order to touch a Weave Cord *within* this school of Majik, the Anchor must be "configured". This configuration is a specific knot of any [Terrum](Terrum.md) material. Selecting a Weave Cord to pull on is often called "knotting an anchor". Now, in order for a Blood life to pull on a Weave Cord, one end of the knot is connected to the core of the anchor and the other has to be connected to a vein, the Anchor usually has a "veinport" that one end of the knot plugs into, the other end of that port is where a vein will be inserted into. The final function of an anchor is to control the flow of blood from that anchor to the knot in the anchor. When it is opened, the blood will flow in and the knot acts as a conduit soaking in the blood and connecting to the Weave Cord associated with that knot. As long as there is blood flowing, the connection from the Weave Cord to the Maji's heart is complete, and allows them to wield the powers of that Weave Cord.

The type of material the knot is made of affects:
1. How much of that power the Majus can pull from the Weave Cord
2. How efficient it was with using the Maji's blood for pulling power from the Weave Cord

Pouring blood on an unbound Weave Anchor has no affect, though interestingly the blood is still absorbed by the anchor.

Since a vein has to grow into the port of an Anchor and an Anchors configuration is that of a knot, the Anchor thus must be embedded or "bonded" into the body of the Majus which requires surgery, and it must have its configuration chamber accessible from the surface of the body so they can switch out knots, and also be able to upgrade the anchor as some may want to upgrade to an "auto-knotting" anchor. These auto-knotting anchors can take a single strand of flexible material (rope, wire of different metals, etc) and then auto knot and knots used for the given Anchor Structure the Maji is bonded with.

It is called a bond as generally once anchored, it is a commitment. If one binds themself with an anchor, it is usually fairly safe to remove it ***within*** a year. This bonding period is capitalized on at academies so students can try out a school of majik and decide what bond to commit to. But once a year or two passes, the body is fully grown and connected with that anchor, removing it will likely kill the person. Artificers aren't sure if there is some dependence the body grows into when healing from a binding of an anchor, or if something else causes these fatalities.

### Binding to Multiple Anchors

Creating an Anchor that can allow usage of bloodmajik in different schools is at this time not possible, current knowledge understands it as needing fundamentally different structures for the anchor between the schools of majik.

Binding oneself to multiple anchors almost always causes death in a matter of months, though during that time they *can* use the anchors. Thus people are only bound to a single Anchor.

Though in history there *have* been reports of suicide assassins who are bound with multiple in an attempt to bring to heel all the powers to take someone down. Unfortunately it usually never goes well for the assassin in those stories, the more they are used, the faster their conditions worsen, making it hard to train and be efficient enough to use all the anchors affectively before dying. Artificers believe there is a conflict that grows between anchors bound to a body for which the body should regrow and heal around after being bound to it, effectively turning that body into a battlefield between anchors fighting for your bodies attention/dependence.

## Detailed list of BloodMajik Spells Developed & Practiced
### Mardic School of Elemental BloodMajik
 Offensive majik. Artificers who specialize in Elemental Anchors worship the [Marda](Marda.md) [Allkin Bond](Allkin%20Bond.md).

 **Fireball (projectile)**
 A single ball of fire. Upon hitting something, a fiery explosion takes place.

 **Flame-breath (area)**
 A continuous stream of fire cast from the anchor.

 **Inferno (area — sustained)**
 A sustained and spreading fire that catches on nearby flammable surfaces. Unlike Flame-breath, the caster does not need to maintain concentration once the fire takes hold. Used historically in warfare to destroy fortifications and siege engines. Many academies debate the ethics of teaching it.

 **Icicles (projectile)**
 Darts of sharp ice fired from the anchor.

 **Frost-breath (area)**
 A continuous stream of frost. Stacks exposure on targets, progressively slowing them.

 **Glacier (target — slow)**
 A sustained cast that builds ice around a target over several seconds, eventually encasing them completely. Used for capture and imprisonment rather than killing. The ice must be maintained — if the casterstops, it melts at a natural rate.

 **Blizzard (area — sustained)**
 Creates a localised storm of driving ice and cold wind in a radius around the caster. Disorienting, damaging over time, and strips visibility. Costly to maintain.

 **Electric Bolt (projectile)**
 A single ball of electrical energy. Upon hitting something, an electrical explosion takes place.

 **Electric-breath (area)**
 A continuous stream of electricity from the anchor.

 **Chain Lightning (projectile — chaining)**
 An electric bolt that upon striking a target, arcs to the nearest living creature within range, and can continue chaining. The damage diminishes with each arc. Effective against clustered targets, dangerousin mixed company.

 **Steam (area)**
 When a Flame-breath or Inferno and a Frost-breath are cast in proximity — whether by the same or different Maji — the resulting collision can be channelled into a scalding cloud of steam. Experienced Elemental Maji can produce this deliberately. It cannot be cast directly; it is a known emergent effect of combining fire and ice majik.

### Rastuic School of Vitality BloodMajik

 Supportive majik. Artificers who specialize in Vitality Anchors worship the [Rastur](Rastur.md) [Allkin Bond](Allkin%20Bond.md).

 **Bloodroot (self/others)**
 The Majus grows a thick, dark-barked root from the ground in moments — what would take weeks in nature taking only seconds through the power of the Rastur Bond. When snapped or cut, the root weeps a deep red sap. The target drinks the sap, which stimulates the body's own blood production at a dramatically accelerated rate. A significant blood loss that the body alone might take four to six weeks to recover from can be restored in a matter of days, or in exceptional cases — with a skilled Majus and a sustained cast — within a day.

 Bloodroot does not replace blood directly. It encourages the body to make more of its own, faster. This makes it far slower than a Duric Majus with a supply of stored blood, but Vitality Maji are considerably less rare, and Bloodroot requires no stored blood — only the ground beneath and the blood in the caster's anchor. The root itself withers within minutes of being grown, and the sap loses potency quickly once exposed to air, so it must be consumed promptly.

 Healers regard Bloodroot as one of the most practically valuable spells in the Vitality school, particularly on campaigns and in villages far from Duric practitioners.

 **Fortify (self/others)**
 Temporarily hardens an ally's body, reducing damage taken for a short duration.

 **Verdant Armour (self/others)**
 Living bark and woven vines grow over the target, forming natural armour. Slower to cast than Fortify but the effect lasts significantly longer and does not require blood to maintain once set. It will shed naturally after a day or two as the plant matter dies.

 **Detoxify (self/others)**
 Grow fungus that emit spores which purge poison or disease from the target's body. The fungus dies shortly after.

 **Mend (self/others)**
 Direct wound closure — knitting flesh, sealing cuts, and encouraging rapid healing of surface injuries. More precise than Bloom. Used by healers for targeted treatment rather than area recovery.

 **Rejuvenate (self/others)**
 Restores stamina and removes the effects of exhaustion. Does not heal wounds but allows a body to perform beyond its tired limits for a period. Popular among long-march soldiers and endurance travellers.

 **Regrowth (self/others — slow)**
 With daily casting over weeks, capable of regrowing lost or severely damaged tissue including, in documented cases, severed limbs. No single cast produces dramatic results. Hospital Maji who specialise inthis are among the most respected in the empire.

 **Symbiosis (self + others — link)**
 Links two living beings so they share vitality. Damage dealt to one is partially distributed to the other. Used by guardian Maji who willingly absorb partial harm meant for a ward. The link can be dissolvedby either party and ends naturally after an hour.

 **Thorn Wall (location)**
 A fast-growing wall of dense spiked growth erupts from the ground. Used as a defensive barrier.

 **Ensnare (location)**
 Roots burst from the ground and wrap around a target, restraining them in place.

 **Carnivore (location)**
 Summons a large stationary carnivorous plant that attacks nearby enemies.

 **Ancient Grove (location — slow)**
 Over several minutes, a cluster of large trees grows rapidly, transforming the terrain. Used in prepared defensive positions and sieges to deny ground, create cover, or reshape a battlefield. Too slow for active combat but devastating as preparation.

 **Bloom (area)**
 Releases healing spores in a radius around the caster, recovering wounds of all within range.

 **Spore Cloud (area)**
 A toxic cloud of fungal spores that disorients, weakens, and nauseates targets. Non-lethal on its own but significantly impairs combat effectiveness. Controversial — some academies class it alongside Plague and restrict its teaching.

 **Plague (target — spreading)**
 A weaponized fungal infection introduced through a target that spreads to others in proximity. Progresses over days, weakening and eventually killing if untreated. The most ethically contested spell in theVitality school. Most academies forbid teaching it outright, though the knowledge exists. It is the subject of an ongoing debate in the empire about whether it constitutes an act of war by definition.

### Cerelic School of Aether Bloodmajik

 Temporal and force majik. Artificers who specialise in Aether Anchors worship the [Cerel](Cerel.md) [Allkin Bond](Allkin%20Bond.md). Aether Maji have one fewer direct spells than other schools, but uniquely can inscribe their spells as Runes — see Runic Spells below.

#### Time Spells

 **Speed Up (self/others)**
 Accelerates the target's movement and reactions for a short period.

 **Slow Down (self/others)**
 Decelerates the target. Can be applied to oneself deliberately — useful for precise actions where reaction time is more important than speed.

 **Rewind (self/others)**
 Reverses the actions and physical state of the target for a brief window. If a rewound target is struck by another Rewind, the effects cancel — you rewound a rewind.

 **Foresight (self)**
 A brief glimpse of the immediate future — seconds at most. The Majus perceives what is about to happen and can respond accordingly. Cannot be sustained; the effort of maintaining awareness of two timestreamscollapses the spell. Scholars argue about whether Foresight shows a true future or only a probable one.

 **Stasis (others)**
 Suspends a living being in time, preserving them in a state of perfect arrest — no breathing, no bleeding, no deterioration. Used medically to preserve someone near death during transport to a healer. Theethical debate around using it on an unwilling target remains unresolved in imperial law.

 **Temporal Anchor (object/location)**
 Freezes a specific point in time for a single non-living object or small area. The object cannot be moved or affected while the anchor holds. Used by artificers for delicate work, and by historians incontrolled conditions to preserve the exact state of artefacts.

 **Chronological Reading (self + object)**
 By holding an object and casting, the Majus perceives impressions of what the object has experienced over time — where it has been, what forces acted on it, whether it has been damaged and repaired. Notprecise enough to reconstruct events in detail but invaluable for provenance, investigation, and archaeology. Cerel scholars consider this one of the most sophisticated spells in the school.

#### Force Spells

 **Force Barrier (location)**
 An invisible wall of force at a chosen location. Blocks physical passage and projectiles.

 **Push (self/others — directional)**
 Applies a directed force to a target, launching them in a chosen direction.

 **Pull (self/others — directional)**
 Draws a target toward the caster or a chosen point.

 **Levitation (self/others)**
 A sustained upward Pull applied to a target, allowing controlled vertical movement. Costly to maintain. Brief flight is possible but tiring.

 **Telekinesis (objects)**
 Manipulate objects at a distance through applied force. Precision varies with concentration. Heavier objects cost more blood. A skilled Aether Majus can use Telekinesis with surgical precision; an untrainedone will be lucky to tip over a chair.

 **Momentum (objects/others)**
 Augments a force already in motion — a rolling object moves faster, a falling blow hits harder, a thrown projectile carries further. Cannot initiate movement, only amplify it.

#### Runic Spells

 An Aether Majus can inscribe any of their non-runic spells as a Rune on a surface, to be triggered by a condition they set at the time of inscription (proximity, touch, a spoken word, a time delay). The number of available Runic spells equals the number of non-runic Aether spells. Runes are the practical expression of the Cerel Bond's domain over planning, intelligence, and foresight — setting a trap that fires perfectly without the caster being present.

#### Duric

 Forbidden majik. Duric Anchors are crafted and taught only within Durian Artificer sects. Artificers who specialize in Duric Anchors worship [The All Mother](The%20All%20Mothers.md) [Dura](Dura.md) and are exceptionally rare. The practice is socially condemned and in many empires outside the [Emberal Empire](Emberal%20Empire.md), explicitly illegal.

 Because all Majik runs on blood, and Dura's domain ***IS*** bloodmajik itslef, a Duric Majus operates on the fundamental fuel of every other school. This is *potentially* one of the many reasons people seek to suppress it — it is not merely dark, it is a structural threat to every other Majus alive.

 **Blood Drain (self/others)**
 Pulls blood from a target. It coalesces in front of the anchor; the user should have a container to capture it. The anchor cannot return what it draws — a container is required.

 **Blood Fill (self/others)**
 Pushes stored blood into a target to restore them. Requires a container of blood close to the anchor to draw from.

 ▎ Note: draining your own blood to fill yourself results in a net loss — you spend blood to draw blood, then spend blood again to return it. Only self-drain if you have no other blood available and an ally urgently needs it.

 **Hemorrhage (others)**
 Causes internal bleeding in a target. Damage accumulates over time and worsens if the target exerts themselves.

 **Coagulate (others)**
 Forces rapid clotting throughout a target's extremities. Slows movement significantly and causes intense pressure pain. Applied to oneself it can stop dangerous bleeding from a wound, though the sensation is described as excruciating.

 **Necrotic Touch (others — contact)**
 Accelerates decay in living tissue at the point of contact. Worsens existing wounds rather than creating clean new ones. A slow, creeping damage that is difficult to treat.

 **Mark (others)**
 Tags a target with a trace of their own blood drawn by the anchor. The caster can thereafter sense the marked target's location passively, through walls and at significant distance, until the mark fades (roughly a day).

 **Puppet (others)**
 Controls a target by directly manipulating the movement of blood through their body, forcing gross motor movement. Short duration, requires concentration, and is visually unmistakable to witnesses. One of the most feared Duric spells. Beings with very little blood or unusual physiology resist it.

 **Anchor Sight (area)**
 Senses all active anchors in the surrounding area, identifying their school and whether they are currently casting. Used by Duric Maji to assess threats and locate other Maji without being detected themselves.

 **Suppress (others)**
 Interferes with blood flow through a target's anchor, preventing them from casting for a short duration. The target feels the disruption but may not immediately understand why their majik has failed.

 **Blood Lock (others)**
 A more complete and prolonged version of Suppress — fully seals the blood flow around the target's anchor, silencing them entirely for up to several minutes. Costly to cast.

 **Drain Cast (others)**
 Siphons the blood cost of a nearby Majus mid-cast, forcing their spell to misfire and costing them additional blood beyond what they intended to spend. Requires the Duric Majus to have Anchor Sight active or awareness of the target casting.

 **Corrupt (others)**
 Briefly corrupts an anchor's attunement, causing the next spell cast from it to backfire unpredictably. The corruption fades if no spell is cast within a short window.

 **Reanimate (others — dead)**
 Temporarily brings a dead corpse back to an animated state to fight for the caster. The reanimated are weaker than in life but retain basic combat instinct. The duration depends on how recently the subjectdied and the amount of blood remaining in the body.

 **Commune (self + dead)**
 Briefly speak with the recently deceased. Works only within hours of death and only while blood remains in the body. The experience is reported as deeply unsettling by those who have witnessed it. This only works if the person is still in the Passing Garden, if they've moved on to the Aether, the commune doesn't work. Durian theologians consider this the closest living proof of Dura's domain.

 **Death Sense (self — passive)**
 Heightens awareness of nearby dying or recently dead creatures. Used for tracking, locating the wounded on a battlefield, or sensing ambushes where the recently dead have been left as markers.

 **Final Breath (self + dying other)**
 Siphons the last life force from a creature at the point of death, healing the caster or empowering the next spell cast. Can only be used on a target that is already dying. Durian theology regards this as an offering freely given by Dura.

 **Death Ward (self)**
 Suspends the caster's death once. The ward absorbs a killing blow and keeps the caster alive at the threshold of death for a short window in which they can act. Shatters after use and cannot be recast until the caster has fully recovered. Among the rarest abilities in the Duric school to execute correctly.

 **Offering (self)**
 The caster deliberately sacrifices a significant amount of their own blood to dramatically empower the next spell. High risk, high result. Regarded by Durians as a literal offering to Dura — asking her for more than the anchor was configured to give.

 **Contract (self + other)**
 A two-person blood pact that links the caster and a willing or unwilling target. Damage dealt to one is partially shared with the other for the duration. Used by Durian guardians to share the burden of harm from a protected person, or offensively — linking an enemy to their own ally so that hurting one hurts both.

 **Tithe (area)**
 Drains a small amount of blood from every living creature within a radius, channelling it into the caster as healing. The victims feel it as a brief weakness and dizziness. Named by Durian theologians as an echo of what Dura asks of the world in exchange for her influence over Majik.
