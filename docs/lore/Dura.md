## Terrum Understanding

Many believe Dura to not be real, most Empires ban the belief and worship of her. People should instead dedicate their blood sacrifices to the true Allkin.

A small sect known as the [Durians](Durians.md) believe in her, and her worships don't require blood sacrifices. Durians namely exist in the [Emberal Empire](Emberal%20Empire.md), they are still shunned for their beliefs and often turned away from businesses, but belief and worship of Dura is not strictly illegal, its more socially enforced in Emberal.

### Fourth All Father

Some [Durians](Durians.md) believe Dura is looking for the 4th [All Father](The%20All%20Fathers.md). Others instead believe she is trying to MAKE a 4th [All Father](The%20All%20Fathers.md). Both believe she is doing it so that she may bond with him and be more accepted. These two sects believe Dura needs the blood sacrifices for this, both camps are of course wildly wrong.

## Reality
A real [The All Mother](The%20All%20Mothers.md) in the [The Woven Universe](The%20Woven%20Universe.md), with her [Terrum](Terrum.md) [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md), she holds domain over Life, Death, Blood in the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md).
