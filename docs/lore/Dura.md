## Overview
- [The All Mother](The%20All%20Mothers.md) of Death - death, night, bloodmajik etc.

Dura is indeed a real [All Mother](The%20All%20Mothers.md) despite most humans refusing to accept her existence since she doesn't fit neatly in their head canon.

## Fourth All Father

Some[Durians](Durians.md) believe Dura is looking for the 4th [All Father](The%20All%20Fathers.md). Others instead believe she is trying to MAKE a 4th [All Father](The%20All%20Fathers.md). Both believe she is doing it so that she may bond with him and be more accepted. But regardless of which camp a Durian falls in, both camps believe she needs blood to do this.

## BloodMajik

[Blood Majik](Majik.md) is a natural force that acts as the umbilical cord between the [Allkin](Allkin.md) and the creatures they created in the physical universe of their making. Dura, not being a true Allkin of the creatures the [Allkin Bonds](Allkin%20Bond.md) created, bore the responsibility of the bridge to and from the their Aetherial world and their children, as well as having the responsibility of converting their dead back to the Aether, the fabric of the universe they created.


The sect of Durians that believe she is searching for the 4th [All Father](The%20All%20Fathers.md) are true, but nobody, not the Allkin, or even us lorekeepers know whether he really exists or not. Dura may indeed search for him for eternity.

However, she is also the All Mother that cares for the children of the Allkin the most. The Allkin are too busy trying to re-create a mirror image of themselves in the real world. Dura sees the narcissism in them all, they can't stop to appreciate and care for the creatures they have made and toy with. She has taken what she could learn from watching them, namely [Rastos](Rastos.md) to build a garden, though it in truth is rather bland and sad to look at as having death be your domain doesn't quite make you a green thumb. But she tried her best to create her "Passing Garden" where dead creatures can take a moment to continue to exist at least in some tranquil form. When they are ready, she releases them to the Aether, the fabric of this world where they can experience the release of oblivion.

The sect of Durians/Artificers who believes [Blood Majik](Majik.md) is also her domain are actually correct. This is not known in the world, only speculated. Even in the lore this is hidden, the truth of it only mentioned here on this page. These believers are the only ones who may also refer to her as the Blood Mother.

However, she doesn't use the blood absorbed by anchors to somehow "search" for the 4th All Father, instead she uses it to try and keep the Passing Gardens alive, it is the only worldly liquid she can extract from the physical world via bloodmajik, and thus is all she has to keep the garden alive from her deathly touch. She allows anchors to tap into the Allkin powers but the price of that exchange is blood, but that blood sacrifice is used to build a welcoming death to all the children of the Allkin.
