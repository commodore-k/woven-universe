A 1-5 player FPS. It will be a blend of vampire survivor + extraction shooter + base building.

**The goal of the game**: Survive as many days as you can.

At night, there will be a constant stream of enemies always coming towards the Village, though depending on mob type, they may prioritize the player closest to them over the village. The spawn rate and scaling factor won’t be quite as dramatic as VS, or at least it will possibly have a slower buildup in the first part of the curve. The theming will also be similar, dark medieval with magic, so players have access to swords, spears, bows, crossbows, and spells. Enemies will be skeletons, bats, warlocks, vampires, werewolves, etc. Each day the waves will grow stronger. This can partly be kept in check by going on quests (as quests usually (but not always) involve killing a Witch, necromancer, etc. (i.e. powerful beings that help spawn the Terror at night). 
  

There will be a set of pre-made characters that users can play as, experience is tied to that specific character for that specific player, regardless of what world or run of that world they are playing in. Runs are merely a means to upgrading that player’s specific character. However, like vampire survivor, while the player can upgrade a characters base starting stats as they level, any inventory items, or additional upgrades are tied to a specific run in a specific world.


---

A player loads into a world. They start in a small town with NPCs and shops. It is a walled in town but with fairly bare bones defenses and NPC guards. In fact, some of the walls are a bit run down. During the day the world is relatively peaceful. But at night the Terror rises.

  

The village needs to be built up, install and upgrade walls, towers with catapult/trebuchet/ballista, and various magical turrets, ammo reserves for turrets, NPC bowman, crossbowman, spearmen, swordsmen, and mages all of varying upgrade levels.

  

Earn gold so that you can request supply caravans:

- Guards
- Building supplies
- Ammo
- NPC replacements (shopkeepers, engineers)

  

Players are heroes not builders. Town engineers are required to build. But as a hero, you have an eye for strategy, so players use a building mechanic to place ghost placeholders for the things they want built, town engineers then set out to build those things. (Think SCVs in Starcraft).

  

Players main focus should be going outside the village to take down generated quest markers. These quests earn heroes coin and experience, and sometimes even enhanced gear. Quest markers regen in the Morning, so if you don’t complete it during the day or night cycle of that day, then it’s gone and you miss it. Players also earn gold in the morning based on how little damage/death was dealt by the Terror to the village.

  

In the beginning, town defense will not be sufficient without players helping protect it. So players will need to quest and fortify during the day, and help defend the village at night. As the players improve the defenses of the village with traps, turrets, walls, and guards, players will have more freedom at night to quest. Naturally its more dangerous, but likewise, the quest rewards are juicier!

  

  

  

Since the village is attacked at night, it should go without saying that the Shops and NPCs of the village can be destroyed and die, respectively. Since shops are how players can upgrade themselves, buy blueprints for more advanced building options, replenish items, and get new gear. AND since the survival of the village lord is paramount (otherwise the run is over) it is important that both the village AND its occupants survive. However, so long as the lord is alive, not all is lost if shops or npcs are killed. However, you will have to rebuild destroyed structures/shops. A calling spell is used to instantly send a message for caravans, information can travel instantly, not supplies! And here you thought we lived in a world of pure magic and wonder… think again buddy.

  

The time it takes caravan will depend on how far the village is from the King’s city, relative to when it was called. This means caravans _may_ end up coming at night, if this is the case, players may have to focus on protecting the caravan so it can get to the village. But it may also come during the day, making it almost a guarantee that it will make it to the village (though bands of enemies do still wander, but at least the supply caravan isn’t trying to wade through a horde like it would at night). 

  

Village “Runs”:

  

A run will continue until the lord of the village is killed. Players must simply protect the village and the lord for as many days as they can.

  

If the host ends the game:

  

- The current state of the run for all players currently in there are saved. This allows players to rejoin the host in the future without losing the state of their character (inventory, and other run specific items).
- Character stats are retained outside of worlds. (Think deep rock galactic or dark tide, or even vampire hunter)

  

If the host boots up a saved run and plays without the other players to the point where the village lord dies, thus ending the run, then the run is gone and all player data with that run are gone. If other players later join the host, it would be for a different run, and thus they would not have their inventory or other run specific items/upgrades from last time they played with the host as that run is gone now. Same if that run still exists, but the host started up a different run save or created a new run on a different map.

  

  

  

Game:

- Player starts game
- Player loads into the lobby world (i.e. The main city, which, in lore terms, is where caravans ship out from to the village players are doing a run in)
- To start a game, a player goes to the king to propose a place where it would be good to expand the queendom to.

- The player is then shown the world map cut up into small counties, some are locked.

- The world will have varying biomes that will have different affects

- The player will select one for a village to be established in (akin to selecting a mission in deep rock galactic)
- To unlock other areas of the world map, players will have to achieve certain things, e.g. keep a village in the snowy mountains alive for 10 days (etc.)

- To pick up an existing game, the player simply opens the world map

- They will see on their map villages they’ve established with the king so far

- If a player left the village (ended the game) but the village lord was still alive, then that is an active run they can pick back up, they will see in the world map that the village icon looks normal and see the number of days it has survived
- If the lord of a village is dead (i.e. the last time they played at that village the game ended due to the lord dying), then the village icon will be one of a burnt/destroyed village, with no day indicator. Selecting on that will effectively restart the village
