
## Overview
This is the Pantheon of this Universe. They are real entities that mettle in the World. The Pantheon is comprised of two groups, [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md) and [The All Fathers](The%20All%20Fathers.md). They each hold domain over different aspects of the universe, But they both share domain over living creatures (this obviously includes humans). And so The All Mothers without the All Fathers, or The All Fathers without the AllMothers, would not alone have been able to create living creatures. And none did for an unknowable amount of time in the universe until the bonding of the Allkin, which gave life to all creatures in the world. They act as indirect sculptors, using evolution as their scalpel after the initial birth of life to craft their children over time.

Rastos: The Father of the Garden, bonded with Urda, the Mother of Earth
- This Allkin bond is often referred to as "Rastur" / "The Rastur Bond"
- Represents peace, joviality, comedy, growth, long life, and happiness

Cerus, The Father of the Sky, bonded with Stel, The Mother of Stars
- This Allkin bond is often referred o as "Cerel" / "The Cerel Bond"
- Represents love, time, intelligence, logic

Vodus, The Father of Seas, bonded with Focia, The Mother of Fire
- This Allkin bond is often referred to as "Vocia" / "The Vocia Bond"
- Represents power, strength, emotion, sailing

Dura: The Mother of Death, bonded with no-one, and is largely believed to not exist, and is severely looked down upon and socially not accepted to worship (though no laws technically bar it at least in the Emberal Empire, the same cannot be said for other Empirs).

Whenever someone is married, they usually dedicate their marriage to one of the Allkin Bonds. It is considered a bad omen to dedicate your marriage to a single AllKin as it may make the other of the Bond jealous and bring bad luck to the marriage.

None dedicate their marriage to Dura, the Mother of Death, and if they do, none make it known. Since most don't believe in her, they think it is foolish and also chalk it up to a bad omen, but believers claim that it wouldn't as she is not in a bond and so there is no other Allkin to become jealous. However, most ignore that, but speaking as I lorekeeper, I can say the believers are onto something there. But what pray tell would her martial blessings bring I wonder? For now, not even us lorekeepers know.

## Notes
Any family with with combinations of an Allkin bond usually has familial history of rituals focused on that Allkin bond. For example, the [Rastuu Brothers](The%20Rastuu%20Brothers.md) are part of the `Rastuu` family. This name was inspired by the Allkin bond of Rastuu & Urda.

Vodus & Focia as a pair are often used to refer to things that are turbulent or to things that don't mix well. (relating to the pair controlling water and fire)

In this world, the term marriage isn't a thing, they use bonding, since it is the same term they use to describe the pairings of their gods. And so, they imitate their gods through their bonding ceremonies (i.e. a wedding) and dedicate it to one of the Allkin bonds.
