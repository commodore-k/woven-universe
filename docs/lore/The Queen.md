The Queen, Luminița Kordona the III,  who is more commonly referred to by her citizens as the Matriarch, is the beloved ruler of the [Emberal Empire](Emberal%20Empire.md). Luminița the III has two siblings, a younger sister: Ruxandra, and her brother, [Cosmin Kordona](Cosmin%20Kordona.md), who is the youngest of the siblings.

Her mother Mirela Kordona, named her after her Great-Grandmother, Luminița Kordona the II, who ruled during the Void Expansion Tragedy. Luminița the II was herself lost to the Void Expansion before the Arch Maji could contain it. Ioana, her grandmother, and her mother Mirela, both spent ruling days trying to claw back all that their Empire had lost to the Void Expansion.
