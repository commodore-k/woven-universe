Believers of the Fourth All-Mother: [Dura](Dura.md). They congregate and worship Dura at small underground communal centers usually built just outside the city, so that it is out of the way and hidden from public view. These places are called [Underhall](Underhall.md)s.

The main Tenet of Dura is that they should reject the using of [Blood Majik](Majik.md) as it causes a sickness on their gods according to [Dura's Whisper](Dura's%20Whisper.md). This is what the majority of Durians practice.
