The second [Era](Era.md) in the Terrible Knights universe, of the Terriverse if you will.

During this time, small towns start forming into larger interconnected groups. Different groupings try to expand to other towns already part of other groups, causing tension and conflict.

Families rose and fell in quick succession.  never able to hold for long. This happened on and off with greater frequencies slowly, we see a dwindling of diverse bloodlines, a consolidation of power. Wars through this Era fuel progress and invention: more advanced handling of majika, and new weapons like bows and iron weapons and armor.

A dark sect of Maji is formed around 2985 as they push boundaries of majika in ways others never imagined.
