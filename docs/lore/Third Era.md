The third [Era](Era.md) in the Terrible Knights universe, of the Terriverse if you will.

During this Era we slowly see the fluxuations of the [Second Era](Second%20Era.md) start to stabilize by the mid 2500s.

2431 - [Emberal Empire](Emberal%20Empire.md) is founded by the [Kordona](Kordona%20Bloodline.md) bloodline.


2500-3007 is considered the Age of Peace and Prosperity. Wars rage, but less often and not so much for land grabs, more often than not, they were small and petty conflicts that lasted like a flash in a pan.


unbeknownst to many, the dark sect of Maji had been toiling away for centuries, building knowledge and understanding of majik that no one knew of. Not even [Dura](Dura.md) herself foresaw this. Majik that could access another Dimension. Bloodmajik was a bridge to the Aetheral plane, blood for the powers of the [Allkin](Allkin.md) but this dimension they were peeking into was not of that plane nor their universe. Whose power were they tapping into? The Allkin, nor the lorekeepers like me even know. Could there be a 4th All Father? Or is it tapping something else that the Allkin aren't aware of? If so, what did Dura miss when she established that system with bloodmajik? Maybe the Allkin aren't as perfect as we thought.

What this world was though, the dark sect was only beginning to understand. up in the snowy mountain ranges on the borders of the [Emberal Empire](Emberal%20Empire.md), a group of these maji were working on a portal summoning spell. Know one knows what happened, but it probably didn't go as planned. The portal was created, but it was expanding, and as it did, it consumed any living thing it touched. Unfortunately for the Emberal Empire, many of its lands were consumed by this.

The Emberal Empire, under the rule of [Luminița Kordona the II](Luminița%20Kordona%20the%20II.md) set her eldest Maji to contain the catastrophe. They had very little knowledge of how to tame the ever expanding void (not knowing it was a portal to a dark dimension). They ultimately had to use a containment crystal, a very rare and powerful element of the physical world. The damage to the empire was done, but the expanding portal was captured into a containment crystal. The Emberal Maji carefully sharded the containment crystal to ensure that it wouldn't be easy for the void portal to be removed from the containment crystal. The shards were then spread out across the land hidden in various locations by knights who, once they hid them, committed suicide to ensure the secret of their location died with them, ensuring this realm was protected. A great sacrifice.


The land affected by the void was and is to this day considered cursed by all people of all empires. However, it was only the living creatures that were consumed by the void, trees, water, land, all still intact. It has taken time for nature to heal, with every living thing, even decomposers like bugs dying, the plants had suffered for a year or two as bugs and creatures of all sizes slowly started expanding into the area, but the nature would take a long time to heal before it would start to get better.
