## Overview

- Player Inventory: A universal inventory that is limitless and can be access be any of that player's characters.
- Character Inventory: A limited sized inventory that is specific to a given [[Knight]] for that player.


Both Player and Character Inventories can hold Supplies, Weapons, Gear, and Unique Items. However, there are no weapon or gear pickups out in the world when a player is on [[Deployment]].

Therefore, this leads to the following Ruleset about things that can exist in the player inventory:
- Players CAN collect [[Coin]], [[Supply]], and [[Unique Items]] out in the world when on Deployment.
- Players CAN buy/trade Unique Items
- Players CAN buy/trade Supply
- Players CAN craft Weapons
- Players CAN craft/buy Weapon upgrades
- Players CAN craft/buy Gear
- Players CAN craft/buy Gear upgrades
- Players CAN buy weapon/gear ammo

Note: A Character's Inventory size *can* be upgraded in that character's specific [[Guild Halls]].
