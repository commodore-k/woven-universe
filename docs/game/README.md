## Terrible Knights

### Overview
This game is about 1-5 Knights of the Realm helping the Queen expand her Queendom. But outside the [[Main City]], hordes of [[The Terror]] roam the land. expanding the queendom is a perilous endeavor. And so, her greatness tasks her Knights with this doomed task of expansion.

A player immediately loads into the Main City where the Queen resides. Players select a county in the world map to establish a village for the realm. They then must build up its defenses, and protect the village from an endless Horde of The Terror throughout the nights. Your goal is to keep the village alive as long as possible.

Knights are free to leave a village at any time. It will essentially be treated like a saved game. That is, it is paused until they return. When they leave they may seek to start a new village, resume defending another village, or restart a village whose last play-through ended due to its lord being killed.

Each world instance is tied to the host player.

### Art & Theme

This is a low poly 3d game (think Nintendo 64 games, but with a bit better effects). The theme of this game is dark medieval with some magic (to help with theming, think of the 2004 movie *Van Helsing*). So it is grimdark, but with a bit of camp. The player perspective is First Person.
