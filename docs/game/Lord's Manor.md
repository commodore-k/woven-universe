## Overview

This is the Heart of a [Village](Village.md). If this is destroyed, a Village is considered lost. If this happens when players are deployed at this village, the players are sent back to the [Main City](<Main City.md>).

Attached to the Lord's manor is the stockpile.

Inside the Lord's Manor is a treasury that acts as the treasury for that village.
There is also a [Logistics Hall](<Logistics Hall.md>) that operates just like the one at the [Queen's Manor](<Queen's Manor.md>).
