This is where the Queen of the Realm resides.

The [[Logistics Hall]] also resides in the Queen's Manor.

Talking with the Queen and other NPCS like her many husbands may reveal [[Realm Quests]].

If the player wishes to reset the game, so that they may start with a fresh Queendom. They may do so through dialog with the Queen, with one or more confirmation exchanges with the player.
