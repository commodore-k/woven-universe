The Main City acts as an in world main menu and lobby. Think Deep Rock Galactic.

When a [Player](Player.md) boots up the game, this is the map they load into. Players can invite friends who can join into the host's Main City. See [Networking](Networking.md) for how many friends can join.


In this location, players can hang out and do nothing or goof off, or they can visit one of the following buildings in town and interact with the NPCs there for a variety of things.


Talk with NPCs, throughout the city to find [Realm Quests](<Realm Quests.md>).
- Tavern Keeper
- The Queen herself
- One of the queen's many husbands
- Shop Keepers
- Stablehands
- Guild members & masters
- Time Maji at the Clock Tower
- Students and Professors of the Engineer's Academy
- Students and Masters of the Maji Academy
- etc.

## Base Buildings
- The [Barracks](Barracks.md): initial spawn and [Knight](Knight.md) selection. Also enables the standard Guard in Company Requests
- The [Queen's Manor](<Queen's Manor.md>): Where players can complete [Realm Quests](<Realm Quests.md>), unlock counties, establish villages, and manage the logistics of the Realm.
- The [Main Gate](<Main Gate.md>): head out to selected village, spawn point when leaving a Village for any reason.
- The [Clock Tower](<Clock Tower.md>): informs players at the city what time of day it is in the day-night cycle.
- [The Engineer's Academy](The%20Engineer's%20Academy.md): Enables you to request engineers (and later even Clockwork soldiers) in Company Requests.

## Unlock-able Buildings
- The [Guild Halls](<Guild Halls.md>) - Where players use special goods to level up base stats
- The [Bazaar](Bazaar.md) - Where players can trade goods for coin or coin for goods
- The [Tavern](Tavern.md) - A place to drink or eat food (certain menu items can give you boons or banes before heading out to a village).
- The [Stable](Stable.md) - Where you can select and upgrade [Mount](Mount.md)s (same as characters and independent of characters)
- [[The Maji Academy]]: Enables you to request Maji in Company Requests, may also provide other unlocks akin to the Engineer's Academy by completing Realm Quests for them.
