
## Starting The Game
- A player spawns in at the [[Barracks]] in [[Main City]].
- The player selects a preferred [[Knight]] to play as before leaving the Barracks
- The player explores the town and sees there are a few locations they can visit as well as large empty areas, the town seems like it may expand to host more buildings in the future.
	- The [[Barracks]]
	- The [[Queen's Manor]]
	- The [[Main Gate]]
- They player makes their way to the [[Queen's Manor]].
- In here they see there is a [[Logistics Hall]] as well as the [[Queen's Hall]].
- They go to the Queen's Hall to talk with their Queen
- She tells you to not come back until you have completed a Realm Quest.
- The player then goes to the Logistics Hall
- Here they can open up the [[World Map]]

## Establishing A Village & Heading out

- The player sees the entire world, all the biomes, and landmarks.
- They also see overlaid on the map various sized regions, Each of these regions are a [[County]] the Realm claims is theirs.
- The player notices most are locked, but a few adjacent to the city are unlocked.
- Each county has a variable number of markers (1-3 depending on the county)
- One county is squarely in a field biome, another sits over that field biome but also a forest biome, the final unlocked county is squarely in the field biome
- The player selects 1 of two locations in the county squarely in the forest biome
- The map pulls up the village details
	- unsettled
	- creatures:
		- ???
	- resources available to harvest
		- wood
		- food
- The player decides to settle a village here.
- The World Map updates with a village icon at that location
- The village page now reads
	- settled
	- creatures:
		- ???
	- exports:
		- 100 wood / day
		- 25 food / day
	- Night 0 Survivability: 30%
	- scheduled imports:
		- none
- The player then selects the village as their destination
- The player exits the Logistics Table and heads to the Main Gate
- They don't stop by the Armory or any other building as they don't have much to their name besides their character's base loadout
- They click March to Village to load the Village map


## The First Village


### Day Time
- The player spawns at the Lord's Manor for the Village.
- It is currently midday
- They look around and see that the village has walls that almost envelop the village but its not complete.
- Next to the Manor, they see there is a stockpile
- They inspect the stockpile and see the amount of each resources it contains
- The player (from a tutorial they went through outside of this example walkthrough) knows that this is the central supply that fuels the construction of any building, turret, or trap for the village.
- The player opens up their map menu and see that it now defaults to the settlement level map, so they can see the village as well as the entire map the village sits in.
	- On this map are indicators of Village Quests.
	- Village quests only last the day and are randomized on the morning of each day.
- The user makes a mental note of a quest to take out a Warlock holed up in the forest near the village.
- Before the player leaves, they go into build mode and place placeholders for walls, a gatehouse, and a set of stairs up to the wall ramparts.
- The Village engineers set out constructing these placements
- Meanwhile the player heads out to the forest to fight the warlock
- By the time the player completes that quest, it is nearing night
- The player notices that there are only 5 bowman, and 2 spearmen
- They go to the Lord's Manor and to the logistics table
- The logistics map opens up and they make a one time request using the gold they earned from the Warlock Village quest for 5 more spearmen.
- The village is very close to the Main City, so they are expected to arrive within the night.
- The player also notices a lumberyard, and two garden houses, these buildings are economic generators for the village. If they survive the night, then at dawn they generate coin for the village this is done relative to the health of the building. So protecting these and keeping them at 100% is important for the money the village generates.

### Night Time

- Sun sets and the Moon Rises, and with the Moon, so does the Terror. Skeletons, undead, and spiders start marching in from the edges of the map as well as from landmarks on the map
- As they near, the NPC bowmen begin firing at the enemies
- Unfortunately, the engineers didn't have enough supplies from the stockpile to completely finish all that you marked out to be built and one part of the wall was still open with your placeholder still there. enemies start pouring through, though this is the first wave on an easy, close to the Main City settlement so its not unmanageable.
- You and the spearmen start to attack
- In the middle of the battle you hear a trumpet sound, you realize this is the sound made when requested guards from the Main City have entered the map
- The player has a moment to open their map menu and sure enough on the edge of the settlement map, they see 5 blue dots indicating guards, and they are marching right towards the village
- However, this is the middle of the night and the horde is endless, but again this is a first wave so it is fairly sparse out there on the map, so the guards aren't immediately overwhelmed
- They make it a good way in as mobs around them are slowly closing in, on them instead of the village.
- The guards eventually get pinned down just outside the village walls as more of the mobs turn towards them
- Because there is a bottleneck into the village, the 5 guards outside lessen even further the stream of enemies into the village.
- Dawn comes and the enemy mobs start to flee, the guards now ignore the fleeing enemy mobs and once again make way to the village.
- One of the original two spearmen have died in the battle, but the village now has 6 spearmen
- The player runs to the logistics table to request stone supplies from the Main city
- The stone arrives within the day and the engineers, seeing there are supplies in the stockpile that satisfy placeholders, set out to construct them
- There is left over supplies and engineers set out making repairs
- The player has successfully fended off the Terror for the night, and completely walled in the city.
- The village 
- The last thing the player does is go to the logistics table
	- They order 5 more bowmen using the village gold earned in the morning from the economic buildings
	- They also order some more stone.
- When that stone comes in, the engineers will be able to make repairs to everything that was damaged.
- Finally, they inspect the Village details:
	- current building health: 100%
	- village supply cost:
		- wood: 100
		- stone: 243
	- village stockpile:
		- wood: 50
		- stone: 100
	- simulated damage: 20%
	- inbound imports:
		- 100 wood
		- 150 stone
	- inbound guards:
		- 5 bowmen
- The player knows that this means that the village will take some damage but barring a World Event, it will survive, and with the goods they have being delivered, the village should be able to repair all 20% of the damage its expected to take in the coming night.
- Satisfied, the player leaves the village since it is day (cannot leave village to the Main City during the night).
