## Overview
The Queendom's treasury is filled from Villages paying their required [[Tribute]]. 

The Queendom's treasury is used for upgrading the Realm:
- improving the quality of segments on the [[Queen's Highway]].
- creating new segments
- upgrading, adding things to the Main City
