The Player will always exist as one of the provided [Knight](Knight.md)s in the game.

The Player has a universal coin purse that is not related to any one [Knight](Knight.md) and exists outside the duration of a deployment.

See more on [Inventory](Inventory.md).

Each Knight has their own inventory. However, in the [Barracks](Barracks.md), the player essentially has a locker, i.e. a universal storage, where they can store other items, allowing items to be shared between their characters. But like with path of exiles II, while any character can hold any item, they must be of a certain level to use it, and when it comes to weapons, some may not be able to affectively use a weapon or upgrade it since they each belong to a specific Guild.
