## Overview
The [[Game Clock]] is an ever present mechanic. While this isn't a true persistent game, The day-night cycle always moves. The Clock Tower in the [[Main City]] reflects the current time of day it is (though players can also roughly determine this by just looking to the sky.

## Pause / Un-Pause Time

The Clock Tower is a massive magical device the [[The Queen]] had erected. It Displays the time of day/night and also has a shrine inside at its base that can be used to freeze the world outside he Main City, so the day-night cycle is frozen, and outside the Main City EVERYTHING is frozen, Shipments, Company movements, enemy attacks, everything. But the NPCs in the Main City go about their tasks as normal.
