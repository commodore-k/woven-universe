
## Overview
When a [Player](Player.md) levels up a [Knight](Knight.md), the Knight's base stats will all improve to some degree. However, without a Guildhall, players will be fairly limited in how much they can improve their Knights.

Each Knight belongs to a specific Guild. Their guild is where they go to spend unique items on further base stat improvements, character specific weapon & gear improvements, as well as a way to craft new guild specific weapons and gear other than the default Knight loadout.
