## Overview
Supply Chains of the Realm is the strategy gameplay of Terrible Knights

When Player's are in the [[Main City]], it is always day, so there is no rush, it is the place in the game that gives players breathing room. They can look at the world map and assess the logistics of the Realm. Players can and should also manage Realm logistics while deployed to a Village as the Day-Night Cycle will resume and World Events may occur affecting villages you aren't at. And any Village you aren't at still experiences the Night cycle but will go through a simulated one since its map isn't loaded (as it is not the village that the players are at). This will happen for all active settlements the player isn't at.

To affectively manage supply chains players need to
1. Ensure each village is somewhat capable of holding its own. Players should thus focus on going to vulnerable villages and playing there until the village is strong enough to defend its self, or at least well enough and coupled with proper supply lines so it can rebuild during the day.
2. They also need to ensure that supply lines are set up. When a player is out at a village, the day night cycle doesn't just run for the village they are at, it runs for ALL villages that are established and alive.


## Shipping Goods & Guards

### Rules
- Players can make a one-shot shipment of goods
	- funding: player coin and/or destination village coin
- Players can make a one-shot shipment of guards
	- funding: player coin and/or destination village coin
- Players can setup a single recurring shipment of goods to a given village
	- funding: destination village coin only
- Players cannot setup a recurring shipment of guards
- Only one shipment of goods can en route to a given village at a time (independent of troop requests)
- Only one order of troops can be en route to a given village at a time (independent of caravans).
- Players can use the shipment priority list to setup the priority list of the goods in the recurring shipment should the village ever not have enough funds for the entire shipment cost (this priority list includes the shipment upgrades, e.g. protection).

So at most a single village can have 1 caravan and one order of troops marching at a time

Note: One time orders come in handy when [[World Event]]s take place at a village that you are (or aren't at).

All Shipments and Troop Requests can be made at a [[Logistics Hall]].

## Shipment

A shipment is a caravan of wagons. There are two dimensions a player has to consider when creating a shipment, the segments used to define the [[Route]] path, and the quality of each segment in that path from the Main City and the destination Village.

A Wagon in a caravan can hold some amount of [[Supply]] and/or [[Ammo]].

The length of the route from Main City to destination Village determines how long the route will take, and the lowest quality segment between to villages in the route will determine the number of wagons that can be in that caravan, thus limiting how many goods can come per shipment to the village from the Main City. Thus investing in the quality of routes in the Realm is another logistical thing for players to keep in mind. But the more wagons in a caravan, the longer it may take, so there is a balancing act to establishing the Village shipment.

If the Route for a shipment becomes invalid (i.e. a settlement that is part of the route is destroyed), then the shipment is not ran until
A. The Settlement is re-established
B. The Route for the Shipment is updated with a new valid Route.

### Shipment Safety
A shipment travels safely on routes UNTIL it enters the village map.

#### Simulated Arrival
If during the day, no problems, the city gets 100%

If during the night, its survivability will be determined by the survivability of the city + and protection upgrades the shipment has

#### Arrival at Village Player(s) Are at

Regardless of time, the survivability will be solely based on how things play out in that game, put the fate of the shipment from the edge of the map to the village in the player's hands. (The protections still play a role of course).



### Shipment Upgrades
By default caravans aren't protected.

Varying levels of protection and upgrades can be applied but each incur a cost. Protections can be setup for recurring shipments as well.

The protection is a trade off if the estimated delivery time falls during the night time.
## Company Request

A Company of troops simply march to their destination.

The speed at which they arrive depends on the percentage makeup of the dispatches "heaviest" unit + the distance the settlement is from the Main City + the quality of the worst segment on the player selected route.

Players will have to play with the balance of route distance + route quality + company makeup.

### Makeup
A company can include any combination of:
- [[Engineers]]
- [[Villagers]]
- [[Troops]]
