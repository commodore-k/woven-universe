## Overview

If [Route](Route.md)s are the veins of the [Supply Chains](<Supply Chains.md>), then supplies are the oxygen carried along it, fueling the [Village](Village.md)s of the Realm.

Supplies are needed to enable Village [Engineers](Engineers.md) to construct [Buildings](Buildings.md), [Turrets](Turrets.md), [Traps](Traps.md).
