Establishing a settlement will result in generating a map for the settlement with a starting [[Village]] placed somewhere towards the center of the map, give or take an independently randomly generated x and y offset.

This action can be taken at the [[Logistics Hall]].

In order to establish a settlement, this will require an upfront cost that ONLY the [[Queendom's Treasury]] can pay for. Thus it is important to ensure you have productive villages so that you can unlock new villages.
