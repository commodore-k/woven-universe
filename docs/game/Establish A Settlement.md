Establishing a settlement will result in generating a map for the settlement with a starting [Village](Village.md) placed somewhere towards the center of the map, give or take an independently randomly generated x and y offset.

This action can be taken at the [Logistics Hall](<Logistics Hall.md>).

In order to establish a settlement, this will require an upfront cost that ONLY the [Queendom's Treasury](<Queendom's Treasury.md>) can pay for. Thus it is important to ensure you have productive villages so that you can unlock new villages.
