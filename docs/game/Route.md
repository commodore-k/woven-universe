A Route is made up of segments. A segment is a path from one settlement to another. A route ALWAYS starts at the [[Main City]] and ALWAYS ends at a [[Village]].

ALL settlements in a route must be active. If a settlement is destroyed, the route is not considered valid until ALL settlements in it are active.

Routes are the backbone of the [[Supply Chains]] mechanic
