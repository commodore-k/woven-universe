

World Event only Trigger once a day at Dawn. Therefore, they only happen when players are deployed, not when they are at the [[Main City]].

World Events Are displayed on the [[World Map]].

World Events Can happen at multiple levels and can stack

World events can
- At World Level (meaning every Village in the world is affected by it)
- At a County level (meaning every [[Village]] in that county is affected by it)
- At a Village level (meaning ONLY that specific Village will experience the event)
