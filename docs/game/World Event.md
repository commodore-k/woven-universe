

World Event only Trigger once a day at Dawn. Therefore, they only happen when players are deployed, not when they are at the [Main City](<Main City.md>).

World Events Are displayed on the [World Map](<World Map.md>).

World Events Can happen at multiple levels and can stack

World events can
- At World Level (meaning every Village in the world is affected by it)
- At a County level (meaning every [Village](Village.md) in that county is affected by it)
- At a Village level (meaning ONLY that specific Village will experience the event)
