
## Overview
Time in Terrible Knights is an ever present mechanic.

Time is not persistent, the game doesn't keep running when you exit, we don't try to simulate time between when you last go on etc.

Time is also not consistent. That is, the day-night cycle does consistently run WHEN you are in the game, however, certain Game events do auto wind the clock forward, so it isn't ***true consistent running time***.

Players can roughly determine the time of day by just looking to the sky to see the position of the Sun or Moon.

## Day-Night Cycle

A Full Day: Dawn to Dawn is 30 minutes.
- The Day is 20 minutes long
- The Night is 10 minutes long

This is the case whether players are on Deployment or back at the Main City.

## Main City

The Main City is meant for players to recuperate after a deployment or a place they are brought back to if they lost a city during the night. This is therefore the main reason our game's time doesn't have true consistency. We want to ensure players have plenty of time to interact with NPCs, and goof off a bit in the Main City, BUT we do want time to be *allowed* to be consistent with the rest of the world in the Main City.

Essentially, we don't want to enforce this time consistency on players when in the [Main City](<Main City.md>). We have real lives and things we may need to do in-between [Deployment](Deployment.md)s. For this reason, when in the [Main City](<Main City.md>), players can go to the [Clock Tower](<Clock Tower.md>) and press the Pause time button. In game lore: the Clock Tower is a massive magical device that can cast a world wide time freeze. Any player in the player party make activate or deactivate at the Clock Tower.

This means players can let time move by like normal in the main City, OR it can pause the day night cycle (NPCs may or may not causally complain depending on the time of day-night cycle the world is paused in). The Main City runs normally (NPCs walk around and can be interacted with, only the time of day is frozen), BUT everything else happening on the World Map is truly frozen.


Finally, regardless of what time in a day a player party leaves a village and regardless of what time during the night players loose a village, when they arrive back at the Main City, it will always be Dawn, i.e. the start of a new day.

This ensures that players who want a more real experience (no clock tower pauses with some time pressure between deployments), they always get at least 20 full minutes in the Main City that is safe day time before the night rolls around.
