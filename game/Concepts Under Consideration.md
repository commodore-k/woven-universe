
## [Main City](<Main City.md>) Upgrades
It may be the case that since the Queendom is starting out small, maybe not all the buildings are there to begin with either. This would increase the importance of increasing productivity of villages to better feed the [Queendom's Treasury](<Queendom's Treasury.md>)

Required Buildings from the start:
- [Queen's Manor](<Queen's Manor.md>)
- [Barracks](Barracks.md)
- [Main Gate](<Main Gate.md>)

Unlocks
- [Guild Halls](<Guild Halls.md>): A place for Knights to better hone their craft
- [Bazaar](Bazaar.md): A place for Knights to buy, sell, or trade unique items and supplies
- [Tavern](Tavern.md): A place for players to give themselves one-time [Deployment](Deployment.md) boons and/or banes.
- [Stable](Stable.md): A place for players to, like barracks do for Knights, unlock and upgrade mounts as well as mount gear.

Being places with NPCs, it naturally also unlocks new sources of Realm Quests.
