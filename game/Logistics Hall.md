## Overview
This area resides inside the [Queen's Manor](<Queen's Manor.md>)

From here, the player can unlock counties if they've completed the [Realm Quests](<Realm Quests.md>) to unlock that [County](County.md). From there, they can [Establish A Settlement](<Establish A Settlement.md>) for the Queen.


This is where players can manage the Realm's [Supply Chains](<Supply Chains.md>).

This is all done through the [World Map](<World Map.md>).

## Locations

Logistic Halls can be found at the [Queen's Manor](<Queen's Manor.md>) as well as any [Lord's Manor](<Lord's Manor.md>) of a Village.
