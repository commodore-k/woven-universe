## Overview
The World Map is the same across all games, it lays out the biomes of the map. Overlayed on top of the biomes is a map cut into various shaped counties.

Over time, the game developers may expand the world to include more biomes and additional counties. Note: that there is no real detail to the world map, it just identifies the general shape of the world, the boundaries of biomes and counties.
(we may or may not rework this to also be generative)

This is not a pure open world, players can only exist in the [Main City](<Main City.md>) map, or in one of the [Village](Village.md) maps.

## Player Interactions

In the Player World Map Menu:
- VIEW: the world map and the info on counties and its villages, including the lock state of a county and what [Realm Quests](<Realm Quests.md>) are needed to unlock it.
- SELECT: An existing village (destroyed or active) as the village to travel to when the approach the [Main Gate](<Main Gate.md>).
- VIEW: Map of an existing Village

When viewing the World Map via the [Logistics Hall](<Logistics Hall.md>):
- UNLOCK: A [County](County.md) IFF all its Realm Level Quests are completed
- ESTABLISH: A new Village in a county IFF it has an empty Village slot.
- - VIEW: the world map and the info on counties and its villages, including the lock state of a county and what [Realm Quests](<Realm Quests.md>) are needed to unlock it.
- VIEW: Map of an existing Village
- REQUEST: supplies and/or Guards to any village
