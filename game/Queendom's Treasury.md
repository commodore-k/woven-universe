## Overview
The Queendom's treasury is filled from Villages paying their required [Tribute](Tribute.md). 

The Queendom's treasury is used for upgrading the Realm:
- improving the quality of segments on the [Queen's Highway](<Queen's Highway.md>).
- creating new segments
- upgrading, adding things to the Main City
