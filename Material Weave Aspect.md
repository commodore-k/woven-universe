## Overview
This [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) was woven into the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md) during its [creation](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) by the [Allkin](Allkin.md).

The Allkin [Urda](Urda.md) later bonded herself to this Weave Aspect with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).

## Aspect

Imbues the ability for matter to exist in the Weave this Aspect is apart of.

## Allkin Weave Cord Use

In the vaccum of Terrum, all matter is solid by default

- Can create/destroy definitions of Matter/elements in the Terrum Weave.
- Can control shape, rotation, movement, position of existing matter in a solid state.
- Can transform existing solid matter to from other types of solid matter (i.e. structure)



## Terrum [Majik](Majik.md) Use

- Can control shape, rotation, movement, position of existing matter in a solid state.
