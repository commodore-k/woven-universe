## Overview
This [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) was woven into the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md) during its [creation](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) by the [Allkin](Allkin.md).

The Allkin [Marus](Marus.md) later bonded himself to this Weave Aspect with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).

## Aspect

Imbues the ability for matter to exist in states other than solid (which is the natural state for all matter in a vacuum) in a Weave this Aspect is apart of.

 e.g. a fluid, gas, or plasma

## Allkin Weave Cord Use

- Can create/update/destroy novel Matter states (that aren't solid) in the Terrum Weave.
	- including transition states to an from each other (including to/from solids).
- Can create/destroy Matter currently existing as a non-solid in the Terrum Weave.
- Can control the movement and flow of matter currently existing as a non-solid

## Terrum [Majik](Majik.md) Use

- Can create Matter in any non-solid state, but it will immediately be effected by the environment it is cast in
- Can control movement of existing matter that is currently in a non-solid state.
