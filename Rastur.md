## Terrum Understanding
This is the [Allkin Bond](Allkin%20Bond.md) name of [Rastos](Rastos.md) and [Urda](Urda.md)'s Bonding.


## Reality
This simply represents [The All Father](The%20All%20Fathers.md) [Rastos](Rastos.md) and [The All Mother](The%20All%20Mothers.md) [Urda](Urda.md) that performed [Weave Cord Splicing](Weave%20Cord%20Splicing.md) to create one of the [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md)s needed for [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md) in the [Terrum](Terrum.md) Weave.
