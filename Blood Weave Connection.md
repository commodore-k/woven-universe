

Any [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md) can use their blood to touch any of the [Spliced Weave Cords](Weave%20Cord%20Splicing.md) that created the [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md)s that define their life force (i.e. Life, Death, Blood). They can also use their blood to touch any unspliced [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md) that are made ***from*** the Composite Weave Aspects that define their life force.

Whatever the blood of a blood-life "touches" in the [Weave](Weave.md), e.g. a Spliced or unspliced Weave Cord or even the [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md) itself, it allows that blood-life to use some amount of that force for as long as their blood is touching it, but in doing so, the blood will be absorbed by the Weave, thus limiting how long a blood-life can tap into the power of what it touches.

If the user stops touching/pulling on the Spliced or Unspliced Weave Cord, and there is left over blood in either type of Weave Cord. The being bonded to either type of Weave Cord will see the residual blood seep into their Weave. The affects blood can have on beings changes depending on the Weave it seeps into.
