

Any [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md) can use their blood to touch any of the Weave Cord Bonds that created the Composite Weave Aspects that define their life force. They can also use their blood to touch any Weave Cords that are made *from* the Composite Weave Cords that define their life force.

Whatever the blood of a blood blood life "touches" in the Weave, e.g. Weave Cord Bond, Weave Cord, or even the [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md) itself, they can use some amount of that force for as long as their blood is touching it, but in doing so, the blood will be absorbed by the Weave, thus limiting how long a blood-life can tap into the power of what it touches.

If the user stops touching/pulling on the Weave Cord Bond or Weave Cord, and there is left over blood in the Weave Cord Bond or Weave Cord. The being bonded to the Weave Cord Bond or Weave Cord will see the residual blood seep into their Weave. The affects blood can have changes depending on the Weave it seeps into.
