Weave Majik simply refers to Performing Majik by tapping into Dura's [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md) that she made for the Blood [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md). Magii can only use this if they use the correct Anchor Configuration AND only use the plasma part of their blood, unkown to them, they are exploiting the Fraying of Dura's Blood Weave Cord to directly tap into the powers of the Warp and the Weft. Something that blood-life is not strong enough to on their own, directly interact with like the [Allkin](Allkin.md) can.
