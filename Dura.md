## Terrum Understanding

Many believe Dura to not be real, most Empires ban the belief and worship of her. People should instead dedicate their blood sacrifices to the true Allkin.

A small sect known as the [Durians](Durians.md) believe in her, and her worships don't require blood sacrifices. Durians namely exist in the [Emberal Empire](Emberal%20Empire.md), they are still shunned for their beliefs and often turned away from businesses, but belief and worship of Dura is not strictly illegal, its more socially enforced in Emberal.

### Durians

From [The Woven Story](The%20Woven%20Story.md), Dura was able to sneak most of her story on a Wind Whisper. Only a single woman heard her tale, but she made sure to spread it, and it eventually grew into the community known as the [[Durians]].

A few [Durians](Durians.md) believe Dura is looking for the 4th [All Father](The%20All%20Fathers.md). Others instead believe she is trying to MAKE a 4th [All Father](The%20All%20Fathers.md). Both believe she is doing it so that she may bond with him and be more accepted. These two sects believe Dura uses the blood used from Duric Majik for this. (both camps are of course wildly wrong). Most of the Durians don't believe that given the ancient Durians said she pleaded that they don't use blood majik. So Duric Magi are relatively rare.
