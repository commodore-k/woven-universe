Invented by [The All Mother](The%20All%20Mothers.md) [Stel](Stel.md), it is the product of [The Fifth Woven Axiom](The%20Fifth%20Woven%20Axiom.md). A Weave cord is a cording of [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md) lines that binds a being from one Weave to a single [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) in a another [Weave](Weave.md). This cord binding allows said being control over that Weave Aspect. For example, Stel, in the Aether Weave, crafted a Cord and bound it to the Weave Aspect for Gravity in the Terrum Weave. This allows Stel to control gravity in Terrum from where she was in the Aether Weave.

You could think of a Weave Cord as a single string on a puppet. But to create a Weave Cord requires the Weave to be "lifted". Lorekeepers aren't quite sure what [Cerus](Cerus.md) meant by this, but in any case, when a weave is held up, one can create a Weave Cord.
