Invented by [The All Mother](The%20All%20Mothers.md) [Stel](Stel.md), it is the product of [The Fifth Woven Axiom](The%20Fifth%20Woven%20Axiom.md). A Weave cord is a cording of [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md) lines that are made from a [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) in a [Weave](Weave.md). They can be created and bound to any being outside the Weave that the Weave Cord is made from and it allows that being to control the single Weave Aspect it was made from. For Example, Stel had multiple Weave Cords bound to her, some controlled gravity, others Light.

You could think of a Weave Cord as a single string on a puppet. But to create a Weave Cord requires the Weave to be "lifted". Lorekeepers aren't quite sure what [Cerus](Cerus.md) meant by this, but in any case, when a weave is held up, one can create a Weave Cord.
