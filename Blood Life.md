Any creature whose life force is powered by blood was created by the [Allkin Bond](Allkin%20Bond.md)s and exists under [Dura](Dura.md)'s domain. Blood life allows the being to be sentient, but does not require it.

A blood life is defined by three [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md)s:
- Life
- Death
- Blood

Life and Death are "Lifetime Composite Weave Aspects": they define the "lifetime" that blood-life can exist within.

Life enables the power to create bloodlife and it defines the beginning of that bloodlife's life.
Death enables bloodlife to die and has domain over all aspects of death.

The Blood is the Life Weave Aspect that defines the material part of the blood-life that lives within that lifetime, it provides the power to control an existing/living blood-life

A blood-life can leverage the [Blood Weave Connection](Blood%20Weave%20Connection.md).
