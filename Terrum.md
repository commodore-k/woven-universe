## Overview

This is the Weave of Existence within [the Woven Universe](The%20Woven%20Universe.md) with which humans live in, and the one the [Allkin](Allkin.md) created during [The Weaving of [Terrum](Terrum.md)](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) using the [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md).
