## Overview

To the mortals in this Weave, it is known as Terrum, to the [Allkin](Allkin.md). Either way, this is the Weave of Existence with which humans live in, and the one the Allkin created and have power over. See [The Creation Story](The%20Creation%20Story.md) to learn about the *Weaving of Terrum*.
