## Overview
This [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) was woven into the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md) during its [creation](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) by the [Allkin](Allkin.md).

The Allkin [Cerus](Cerus.md) later bonded himself to this Weave Aspect with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).

## Aspect

Imbues the ability for the passage of time in a Weave this Aspect is apart of

## Allkin Terrum Weave Cord Use

- Can create/update/destroy definitions for how time affects anything that exists or can exist in the Terrum Weave
- Can reverse/forward/pause time within a relative area
	- everything within that defined area will have its temporal affects based on its temporal definition in the weave respectively reversed, forwarded, or paused.
