### The Beginning of the Universe
Our Universe was born through some unknown means. At its birth, there was only one [Weave](Weave.md) of existence: the [Aether](Aether.md). And in this Weave resided beings of great power: The [Allkin](Allkin.md). The Allkin are made up of [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md): Stel, Arda, Urda, and Dura; and [The All Fathers](The%20All%20Fathers.md): Cerus, Marus, and Rastos. These being are powerful and immortal, but their Weave defined a world that contained nothing but the Allkin, a near Void. There is thus no life or death in the Aether Weave for the Allkin.

### The Weaving of [Terrum](Terrum.md)

The Allkin merely existed in their void-like weave, with nothing but their own existence to occupy their time. During this unknowable length of time, they slowly learned what they could do with their power. In a void-like weave, they found there was nothing for them to control. What they did find was that they could touch and wield the [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md), the two fundamental forces of [The Woven Universe](The%20Woven%20Universe.md). Over untold amounts of time, they worked out what they could and could not do with the Warp and Weft, the [Woven Axioms](Woven%20Axioms.md):

 Because of the [The First Woven Axiom](The%20First%20Woven%20Axiom.md) of [The Woven Universe](The%20Woven%20Universe.md) that they learned of, the Allkin were prevented from being able to mutate their Weave to create or change things in it. They learned that they _could_ distort their own Weave, but in one like theirs where it was a void, it was near meaningless, save to quickly travel distances in said void. This was established as [The Second Woven Axiom](The%20Second%20Woven%20Axiom.md). However, after many years of further tinkering with the Warp & Weft, they discovered that they *could* create ***other*** weaves of existence AND those weaves ***could***  be mutated and changed. They codified these two findings as [The Third Woven Axiom](The%20Third%20Woven%20Axiom.md) and [The Fourth Woven Axiom](The%20Fourth%20Woven%20Axiom.md), respectfully. The catch with the latter two axioms is that it takes an **immense** amount of power to *properly* do this. When these were still theories they were trying to prove, they figured with all 7 of the Allkin, they could manage the creation of a new Weave and be able to mutate it. If they were successful in this, they may have a way to live for something more than drifting in this void of a Weave for eternity. They were of course successful, and those two axioms were thus established. They called the Weave they wove from their experiment [[Terrum]].

Because it took all 7 of the Allkin to reliably use the Warp and Weft to create or even interact with the Terrum Weave after its creation, the Allkin desired a way to more easily mutate and control the Terrum Weave without all having to congregate together and lift the Terrum Weave to mutate it in the way they wished. [Stel](Stel.md), one of [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md), had come up with the idea of [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md). The ability to create Weave Cords introduced [The Fifth Woven Axiom](The%20Fifth%20Woven%20Axiom.md).

And so, all 7 Allkin lifted up the *Terrum* Weave. They then took turns relinquishing their hold, with only six Allkin now holding up the *Terrum* Weave while the one worked on cording their Weave Cords.

 [Dura](Dura.md) (one of [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md)) was the last Allkin to Cord her own Weave Cords, she saw that the others had already made multiple cords that together controlled all [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md)s of the *Terrum* Weave. Dura tried to make a Weave Cord for one of the Weave Aspects of Terrum even though there was already a Weave Cord for it. Dura found that she couldn't. It was then that the Allkin learn of [The Sixth Woven Axiom](The%20Sixth%20Woven%20Axiom.md). Once a Weave Cord is made for a given Weave Aspect, another cannot be made. Rastos (one of the [The All Fathers](The%20All%20Fathers.md), who was in a bit of a love triangle with Dura and Urda (another of [The All Mothers](The%20All%20Mothers.md)) at the time tried to unbind one of his Weave Cords for Dura... but this is when they learned a horrible new truth. Once one binds a Woven Cord to themselves, the Cord can never be fully undone, nor can it ever be unbound to the being it was bound to. This came to be [The Seventh Woven Axiom](The%20Seventh%20Woven%20Axiom.md). In Rastos's attempt to undo his cord, he did discover one more Axiom. While the Seventh Woven Axiom prevents you from undoing a cord completely, one can still modify their cord slightly. Nothing that would ultimately change what Weave Aspect it controlled, but enough that it left open some room for imagination. This was [The Eighth Woven Axiom](The%20Eighth%20Woven%20Axiom.md).

With nothing left to do, they all set down the *Terrum* Weave, leaving Dura as the only Allkin with no Weave Cords.

## The Bonding Of The Allkin

### Dura's loneliness

For billions of [Terris](Terris.md) years the Allkins used their cords to mold the Terrum Weave to their liking, learning how to wield their cords effectively along the way. Dura, having no Cords to use was left out. She would try to speak with her Kin, but they often ignored her. They would tell her they needed to concentrate and could not be distracted. Even Rastos was, like the other Kin, enthralled in the molding of Terrum and told her he couldn't be distracted, he needed to keep up with the others.

Before the Weaving of Terrum, they had been alone in the Void yes, but they had each other, and Dura longed for that time again, for now she found herself alone, left with nothing to do but wander the void and watch Terrum be created.

The work that Rastos and Urda did on various planets mesmerized Dura. She marveled at the great trees and vast plains of flowers and various other types of plants on the varied terrain Urda created on the planets Stel molded for them.

### The Blood Life Plot

But after all of this time and work, the 6 Allkin still felt dissatisfied. At the end of the day, they were merely on lookers into this Weave, but they couldn't *experience* it.

The 6 Allkin, reviewing their learned [Woven Axioms](Woven%20Axioms.md), came to a series of realizations. Their existence is of the Aether Weave. Because of [The First Woven Axiom](The%20First%20Woven%20Axiom.md), they cannot mutate it. However, the Aether is a Weave, and thus it has its own set of Weave Aspects. Thus there MUST be a set of Weave Aspects in the Aether Weave that allow for the Allkin's sentience and abilities. If they can create sentient beings such as themselves in the Terrum weave, then those beings could mutate their Aether Weave (thanks to [The Fourth Woven Axiom](The%20Fourth%20Woven%20Axiom.md)). If they could do that, beings of Terrum could fill their Void Weave with planets and Stars as they did for the Terrum Weave. The Allkin would finally have a universe they could ***experience***. But it would also mean those beings would have the power to destroy their weave. But the Allkin also have power to destroy Terrum, perhaps they could both exist together due to mutually assured destruction? That could be too risky though. They then thought: What if they could create life that was sentient, but less powerful. Even better, if the Allkin could *control* them, then by proxy, they could be the ones to mutate their *own* Weave without violating *ANY* of the Woven Axioms.

Once again, it was the All Mother [Stel](Stel.md) that came up with the potential solution. Through her studies of the Weave, she formulated the idea of [Weave Cord Splicing](Weave%20Cord%20Splicing.md) which was possible thanks to [The Eighth Woven Axiom](The%20Eighth%20Woven%20Axiom.md), to make [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md)s. She theorized that by doing this, they could make these more complex Weave Aspects in the Terrum Weave that would allow for them to create what she called [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md). Her theory was that with blood life, they should be able to create sentient beings from it that are much less powerful than them such that on their own, the blood-life cannot touch the Warp and Weft ensuring they cannot modify or destroy the Aether Weave. Further, if they create Weave Cords for these new Weave Aspects that allow for the blood-life, then they would hold domain over the forces of said blood-life. And if they did this, then they could *control* the bloodlife. And importantly, when they control the blood life, they could channel their powers through to them such that the blood-life *could* touch the Warp and Weft, thus giving the Allkin the control they would need to mutate their own Aether Weave.

If the Allkin were going to make these Weave Cord Bonds, they would need all 7 of the Allkin to lift up the *Terrum* Weave again. This meant they needed Dura...

### The Plead For Dura

The Allkin came to Dura who was a bit caught off guard having been left out of just about everything for ages. They started by apologizing to her for how they had ignored her for all this time. Marus had convinced Rastos to do most of the apologizing portion. They then explained to Dura that they found a way to change their own weave and with this, they wouldn't have to be stuck in a void Weave, thy could make it their own! Dura felt hope claw up in her mind, she asked them how they could possibly do this given the [Woven Axioms](Woven%20Axioms.md) prevented this, and if this would mean they could spend time together again. They waved off the second part with an "oh of course, but there was plenty of work they'd have to do first" sort of explanation. They then explained that they would make sentient life in Terrum, something Stel called Bloodlife. This bloodlife isn't not strong enough on their own to touch the Warp & Weft, but when controlled by the Allkin, they could be. And to do this they would create these Composite Weave aspects that allowed for this Bloodlife and then create Weave Cords from it. From there they would use the Weave Cords to make and control the sentient life to, in turn, modify and create things in their own Aether Weave. And they couldn't do it without all 7 Allkin as they need to be able to lift up the Terrum Weave so that they can splice their Weave Cords to create these new Composite Weave Aspects in the Terrum Weave. Thus, she was the most important piece of this project, without her, they wouldn't be able to lift up the Terrum Weave. Four Allkin would be too few to keep it up with the other two busy splicing their Cords, even the 5 holding it were going to be under quite some strain. Cerus had thrown in: "And your the strongest of us, holding the weave with you will make it a bit more bearable". He wasn't wrong.

As Dura listened to them explain all this, her interest in the plot turned to worry, they were going to create this sentient Bloodlife and then enslave them with new cords that controlled the powers of said Bloodlife. They would make these sentient beings a tool and nothing more, they would have no free will unlike the Allkin. And on her end: not that she wanted to control sentient beings, but would they even consider letting her have these new weave cords? She wasn't included in the making of this plan, and they ignored her all while making Terrum, why would they let her have these cords now for the most important part of this whole plot. And how long would they spend on this? And would it even ultimately satisfy them? How much longer would she be alone? Sure Terrum looked beautiful, but even if they made the Aether Weave as beautiful, what good is it to sit in a beautiful garden when you are alone for eternity? She'd still throw this all away if she could just have her kin back the way they used to be, if she could spend more time with who Rastos was those many ages ago. She also feared Rastos was falling more for Urda with all the time they spent together working on the Terrum Weave, and this project would probably only continue that. Yes, the work on Terrum and this plot had changed them she thought, they didn't seem quite the same.

She decided she would help, but secretly, she had a plan of her own.

## Theft of The Bloodlife Cords

And so, all 7 Allkin used their combined powers to lift the *Terrum* Weave, and as each pair took turns relinquishing their hold on the *Terrum* Weave to splice their weave cords, the other 5 held it up under the immense strain that befell them. Splicing Weave Cords to create these new Composite Weave Aspects was a long and arduous process, but the Composite Weave Aspect was usually created halfway through the splicing process, the rest of it was tying off the strands of the splice to re-secure the Spliced Weave Cords. And to hold up a Weave takes great concentration. So when each Allkin pair made it to the part in the process where the Composite Weave Aspect was created in the Weave, Dura started work on cording a Weave Cord for that aspect, capitalizing on the fact that no one would see as they all had to focus on their tasks at hand. Trying to do this was immensely difficult for Dura and pushed her to her absolute limits but she persisted, determined to capture these cords for her plan. When the first two: Cerus and Stel, had finished, Dura had just completed her Cord for the First Composite Weave Aspect moments before, this one was the *Life Weave Aspect*.
The next two were Rastos and Urda, Dura had to push her self, last time was too close. Again, When Rastos and Urda finished, Dura had not improved, finishing her Cord mere moments before they had finished their splicing, so thankfully no one had yet noticed what she was doing. This second cord was for the *Death Weave Aspect*. Marus and Arda were last, Marus and Arda were really skilled with Cording, Dura worried she wouldn't finish in time. As soon as the Weave Aspect was in the Weave, Dura set to work, pushing herself to what felt like past her own limits,  feeling an immense surge of pain. She completed the cord, this one being for the *Blood Weave Aspect*. She noticed part of the cord was frayed, if she didn't fix it now it would always be frayed, but it was successfully bound to her, so she knew the cord was solid, suddenly she felt the strain of the Weave lessen... Marus & Arda had finished and had picked up their parts of the Terrum Weave, it was too late, she wouldn't be able to fix it.

With the splicing process completed, the 6 Allkin moved onto the last step of the process: to create the new Weave Cords from the Life, Death, and Blood Weave Aspects which would give them domain/control over them, thus allowing them to create and control [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md). The group had chosen Marus to take on these cords, he relinquished his hold on the Terrum Weave and set out to make the Cord for Life. But it was then that he realized he couldn't create a cord from it. He knew from [The Seventh Woven Axiom](The%20Seventh%20Woven%20Axiom.md) that meant a cord already existed for it. He panicked. He tried to make one for the Death Weave Aspect, it didn't work either, The Blood Weave Aspect next, no dice. They were all already bound? He looked up to his kin holding the Terrum Weave. Looking at each of them, he saw them all with their cords. Wait, they ALL had cords. He looked back to Dura. There he saw 3 cords bounded to her. He yelled out to his other kin to put down the Terrum Weave, They all set the weave down, Marus and Dura locked eyes. Marus proceeded to tell them what Dura did, pointing at her for them to see.


The 6 Allkin were frustrated, but then they tried to salvage the situation, in part to calm themselves and in part sure that Dura was on board with the plan. They figured they could help her learn how to use cords easy enough, the plan could still work. Dura however, refused. Dura said she would not enslave the life they would create for their own desires, the kin grew more and more frustrated as they argued back and forth with her. Rastos was as frustrated as they were to start, though he hadn't said anything. And when Rastos finally looked at Dura and they met each others eyes, the spell of the group on him broke and his frustration waned, she could see it in his face. But Rastos didn't move to defend her, he instead stayed quiet, only now looking saddened. It was then Dura realized she was to be alone in this fight.

Urda said "You say you don't want to 'enslave' them like us yet you steal domain over them? Sounds to me like you just want to control them yourself".

Dura looked around at them as she said "I don't quite look at it as stealing Urda, more like taking what's owed to me. And no I won't control them, I took domain of these Weave Aspects to prevent you all from using them. These are my Weave Cords and I can do as I wish, and for me, I will abstain from using them. If Bloodlife emerges in Terrum, my gift to them is freedom from your control, free will." her gaze ending with Urda.

Dura then walked by Rastos and whispered in his ear "That hurt Rastos, but I can only hope you see my reason in this. If you ever want to leave them, this is how you can fold to where I plan to go" - she then told him where, then using the Warp and Weft, she folded away.

Urda rolled her eyes, she went up to Rastos and asked him what she'd said to him, Rastos said she was just saying how disappointed she was in all of us and wanted to say she wished she could stay for me. Stel looked at Marus "We don't need her, we can still make this plan work". Everyone looked to her puzzled. This caught the attention of Urda and Rastos just as they had finished their exchange.

We're just going to have to coax Bloodlife out of the Terrum weave and evolve it to sentience the hard way. This is going to take a while, a long while." 

## The Whispering Winds

The 6 Allkin set to work on a planet they called [Terris](Terris.md) in the Terrum Weave. And over the course of what would be billions of Terris years, the 6 Allkin toiled away, using their cords of the Terrum Weave Aspects to try and create Bloodlife. It took ages, but they eventually did it. They had created a rudimentary creature and they were able to nudge and guide the evolution of the bloodlife that came from that creature in to what they'd set out to originally create, a sentient creature the called: Humans. The Allkin were estatic, the first part of their plan was complete.

While The 6 Allkin knew this was a great milestone, humans were too weak to be able to directly touch the Warp & Weft like they could. Had they had control of the Bloodlife Weave Aspects, this would have been the stopping point. No Bloodlife can touch the Warp & The Weft, but they can be bred to get close to that power. Humans were nowhere near the peak of what bloodlife could become. But humans were the first piece of the puzzle, as they unlocked sentience which was one of the requirements to be able to touch the Warp & Weft.

Since the Allkin couldn't control them directly with the Weave Aspects of the bloodlife. They would have to try another form of control. Because of this, the Allkin needed to ensure that humans were the right kind of sentience, one that is malleable, gullible, obedient. If they were, then they wanted to ensure the Allkin where embedded in their minds from the beginning, to ensure they didn't grow independent and apart from them. If they were to control them with words, they'd need to be embedded in their culture from the beginning as they worked to evolve them into greater beings.

Cerus, having Cords that can control the Winds of Terris knew he could use his cords to convert the vibrations of his voice into the sounds of the wind, through this, he and his siblings could communicate to the humans.

With this ability, they hatched a plan, they would spin a tale, most of it truth to gain their trust, but with just enough lies to lead them down a belief system that would make them believe in and carry out their commands. Ultimately, it would be up to the humans to be able to hear their voice in the sounds of the wind and to also believe it. If they didn't, then the creation of these creatures would have been a failure. The Humans didn't disappoint. See the [The Creation Story](The%20Creation%20Story.md) for more details.



## The Blood Addiction

After the humans proved to the 6 Allkin, Cerus teaches the humans about [[Majik]] and how to use it. When the first humans start to pull on the cords with their blood to use this "Majik", i.e. the power of the Weave Aspect that Cord is made from, they see residual blood seep into their Aether weave, wicking off of the Weave Cord bonded to them.

overtime the 6 Allkin learn to manipulate the blood as more comes in from more humans using Majik, they can eventually put it into different states of matter, solid, liquid gas, they can change the density, simple things but not much more as it must ultimately stay as blood. The density allows them to give the blood different shades of red too.

And over that same time, they fall prey to their own stories they've spun, pretending more and more to be the gods they say they are to the humans.

They get so into their own creation story they've spun, that they start using the control of the blood they've accrued in their own weave to turn them into solid sculpted bricks they called bloodstone: dense bricks of blood they've collected from their children, i.e. the humans. And with this, they build a Pantheon from this bloodstone.

Urda decides to create a fountain of blood in the center, with deep dark bloodstone border, and the fountain spout looks like thin obsidian but if it instead had a red tint to it. Muddy but still glass like and semi-transparent. In it flowed blood. Marus, mesmerized by it walked up to touch the fountain, letting the running blood chase through his fingers before falling back into the pool. He cocked his head, drew his hand back and up to his lips, hesitated, and then took a taste. He collapsed, and then fell into a hallucinogenic high, the rush was like nothing he ever experienced, the things he saw, we lorekeepers couldn't even comprehend. All too soon the high ended for him. he didn't know what to do, he'd never experienced something like that, and he had lived a very, very long time. He just sat there, and stared at the fountain for a while.

Arda later found him sitting there, she asked him what was wrong and he said nothing for a long moment. but then, he slowly turned up to her, then looked down at his hands. Arda's gaze followed his, she saw his bloodied hands, and then she looked at the fountain. She asked "My bonded love, what's wrong? Is everything okay?".

Marus stood up next to the fountain, he dipped his hand into it cupping some blood in it. He turned to Arda and said "You have to try this blood my love, it is unlike anything I've experienced".

Arda was puzzled, "you... you drank the blood of our own children?"

"Arda, please just try it, you will experience things you never have before" said Marus, his voice hastening as he moved towards her

Arda starting feeling uneasy "You sound unwell Marus, I don't like this from you, stop this, put down that blood, please you're worrying me". Marus came towards her, Arda stepped back, "Marus, stop this seriously!"

Before Arda could react in time, Marus threw the blood into her face, forcing the blood onto her. A good amount got into Arda's mouth, she had winced and stepped back from the attack, but after another moment her eyes shot opened and she too collapsed. Arda proceeded to have a very similar experience as Marus. When Arda came to, she didn't know how to feel, she was mad at Marus, but he was gone, she didn't know where he went, but at the same time, he wasn't wrong... the blood, it made her *feel* something. It, it was amazing. But she felt so wrong for it too. drinking the blood of the blood-life they created, they're children. They were meant to elevate their children, guide them to help remake their own Weave.

Over a short period of time Marus had gotten them all to taste the blood and experience it. Cerus, Urda, and Stel were all apprehensive at first, but willingly tried it and fell in love with it like Marus did. Rastos was the last he visited, and Cerus, Urda, and Stel were with him. Rastos, like Arda was concerned, it felt wrong. Rastos was even more defenseless, the 4 Allkin forced it onto him as Marus did to Arda.

After some time had passed, Marus felt a bit of a craving for the blood, he'd been so busy sharing it with the others that he hadn't had time to enjoy it again. And so he went to the fountain of blood to have another taste, it was wonderful.

Arda, being disgusted with the others, but she had a horrible realization creep in, she was starting to crave for the taste and experience again. She tried to abstain from taking it. Cerus, Urda, and Stel soon had the same feelings come over them, but like Marus, they didn't find a problem with the feeling and simply had another drink. When the craving crept in on Rastos after a time, he had similar feelings that Arda did. As Rastos paced the Pantheon to try and keep his mind off this craving feeling, hs saw Marus speaking with Arda, convincing her that it wasn't wrong to drink the blood, the craving in Arda was however doing most of the convincing, Marus was just pushing her over the edge.

## The Tragedy of Dura & Rastos

Rastos, struck by a memory, recalled that Dura had told him how to reach her if he ever wanted to leave. This craving feeling didn't feel good, and all the other Allkin *seemed* to be of a different mind. And so, Rastos folded the weave to move himself to where Dura said she would be.

Rastos found her floating serenely in the void. he stayed there a moment in the silence, looking at the peace and tranquility on her face as she floated there with her eyes closed and a slight smile on her face, he had a feeling of jealously creep over him, he wished he could be in such a state with this craving nagging at him; what was she doing anyway? He put that out of his mind and called to her, when her eyes opened, they both looked at each other, with the craving pulling on him, he didn't know what else to do and started to cry.

Dura looked at Rastos, concerned and worried about what could have happened. He told her everything. He apologized over and over for how he ignored here during the ages they worked on the Terrum Weave with their Cords. He apologized for not standing up for her when she claimed the Bloodlife Cords. And he aplogized for never following up on her offer until now. He told her he was afraid of the pain this craving for the blood instilled in him when he didn't drink it after a time. Dura wasn't sure what he was talking about, the apologies, blood craving? He was talking all to fast. She asked him to slow down and start from the beginning. And so he did.

He told her everything, the creation of bloodlife, the evolution to humans, the Blood Pantheon, and the blood drinking that caused weird behavior in the Allkin where the forced it on him, and the craving that it was paining him with now. He was surprised when Dura had looked puzzled about the bloodlife and humans. He asked her why she was not watching the Terrum Weave, and that is when Dura told him how she, in her isolation had been working on a state of stasis, a way to experience oblivion despite not being able to die. He mentioned how humans actually did something similar which intrigued Dura.

A part of Dura still wanted to love Rastos, and so she told Rastos that he could stay with her and that they see if they could resolve this craving affliction he had, maybe the stasis technique she'd perfected could help him, she'd just have to teach him how. And so they stayed together for a time. Dura tried to teach him how to go into the stasis state, hoping that it would help Rastos recover. But with the craving, it was hard for him to focus. She felt like he was making good progress despite the affliction, reminding him that it took her ages to get it down, but that was her trying to figure it out on her own. It would take time, but she believed she could help him get there much faster.

But for Rastos, the craving wasn't seeming to get better, it was getting worse, it was like this throbbing pain that was trying to pull him to it, he didn't understand. He was getting short and frustrated with Dura, part of him could tell he was changing, he knew it wasn't good, but it was hard to stay focused on this stasis thing of Dura's AND try to be himself. At some point, he couldn't take it anymore, he *needed* that blood. And so, when Dura wasn't looking, he folded back to the Pantheon, which was built in the same place in the void the Allkin had always resided at.

Dura turned around as she heard the fold, and saw it close just as she turned. She didn't know how to feel for a moment. She wasn't sure why he left and if he was coming back, but she knew deep down why, she just didn't want to accept it. She new he was struggling with something they'd never faced, but part of her was hurt, he was leaving her... again, for the second time. She decided to push those thoughts aside, she would go back for him, save him.

She folded back to their original void space as she figured that's where they'd be. When she arrived, she was taken aback for a moment by the bloodstone pantheon they had built. She knew of this place from what Rastos had told her, but seeing it first hand was... a lot to take in. She could touch something that was not an Allkin... *in* the Aether Weave. She shook herself back to focus. She had to find Rastos. She was sneaking around the pantheon when she overheard some voices. It was Marus, Cerus, Stel, Urda, and Arda. They were all talking about a new story they needed to tell the humans.

They spoke of how they needed to increase the amount of blood they received from the humans and were talking about how to convince them to sacrifice more blood. They needed a bigger blood flow so they could build out a world in blood and have more for drinking of course.

It was Cerus's voice "When they bind the anchor, they can only give so much blood, any more and they'd kill themselves. Further, they only use those bound anchors on them when then need to use the power of one of our cords. If we can give them a reason they should erect unbounded anchors and give blood to it for nothing in return, then more people could give blood.
