## Overview
This [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) was woven into the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md) during its [creation](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) by the [Allkin](Allkin.md).

The Allkin [Stel](Stel.md) later bonded herself to this Weave Aspect with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).


## Aspect

Imbues the ability for a gravity field to exist in the Weave that this Aspect is apart of.


## Allkin Weave Cord Use

- Can create/update/destroy definitions for how gravity works in the Terrum Weave
- Can temporarily apply gravity of some level of pull to a point in space and time outside of the definitions, can last as long as using Weave Cord for this.
## Terrum [Majik](Majik.md) Use

- Can temporarily apply gravity of some level of pull to a point in space and time outside of the definitions (duration lasts as long as pulling with blood)
