## Overview
This was a Composite Weave Aspect that was created by the Allkin [Cerus](Cerus.md) and [Stel](Stel.md) in the [Terrum](Terrum.md) [Weave](Weave.md).

Cerus used his [Motion Weave Aspect](Motion%20Weave%20Aspect.md) and spliced them with Stel's [Time Weave Aspect](Time%20Weave%20Aspect.md) during the [The Bonding Of The Allkin](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Bonding%20Of%20The%20Allkin).

Dura crafted a bond to this [Composite Weave Aspect](Composite%20Weave%20Aspect.md) with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md) during her "[Theft of The Bloodlife Cords](The%20Woven%20Story.md#Theft%20of%20The%20Bloodlife%20Cords)" thus giving her domain over this Weave Aspect in Terrum.
