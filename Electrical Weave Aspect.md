## Overview
This [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) was woven into the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md) during its [creation](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) by the [Allkin](Allkin.md).

The Allkin [Marus](Marus.md) later bonded himself to this Weave Aspect with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).

## Aspect

Imbues the ability for Electricity to exist in a Weave this Aspect is apart of.

## Allkin Weave Cord Use

- Can create/update/destroy electrical conductivity definitions for existing matter definitions in the Terrum Weave
- Can generate and control shape and flow of electricity
- Can control the shape and flow of electricity
- Can absorb existing electricity

## Terrum [Majik](Majik.md) Use

- Can generate and control shape and flow of electricity
- Can control the shape and flow of electricity
- Can absorb existing electricity
