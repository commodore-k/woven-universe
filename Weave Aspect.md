
A ***Weave Aspect***, or an *aspect of a Weave*, is a "force" that exists *in* that Weave. It is not a fundamental force like the [Warp & Weft](Warp%20&%20Weft.md), it is rather a force that is *defined* by that Weave of the Warp and the Weft. So it is a force that only exists in that [Weave](Weave.md) and allows for various things to exist.

[Allkin](Allkin.md) discovered that Weave Aspects can be controlled by a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).

Example Weave Aspects (non-exhaustive, there are many):
- Light
- Gravity
- Plants
- Fire
- Earth
- Life
- Death
- Blood
