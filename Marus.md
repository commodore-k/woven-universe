Marus is one of the three [The All Fathers](The%20All%20Fathers.md), he bonded with [Arda](Arda.md) during [the Bonding Of The Allkin](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Bonding%20Of%20The%20Allkin) which, in [The Creation Story](The%20Creation%20Story.md), is defined as the [Marda](Marda.md) [Allkin Bond](Allkin%20Bond.md).

Marus created [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md) that bonded him to the following 2 [Terrum](Terrum.md) [Weave Aspects](Weave%20Aspect.md), thus giving him domain over them:
- [Electrical Weave Aspect](Electrical%20Weave%20Aspect.md)
- [Fluidic Weave Aspect](Fluidic%20Weave%20Aspect.md)
