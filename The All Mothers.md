The "Goddesses" in the Pantheon of [The Woven Universe](The%20Woven%20Universe.md). 

The All mothers are the life bearers, the world builders, and they bring warmth. Without them there would be no stars, earth, or fire, for these are the domains of the All Mothers.

Without the All Mothers OR [The All Fathers](The%20All%20Fathers.md), there would be [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md).

- [Stel](Stel.md): The Mother of Stars - Stars, light, planets, etc.
- [Urda](Urda.md): The Mother of Earth - earth, stone, etc.
- [Arda](Arda.md): The Mother of Fire - fire, heat, etc.
- [Dura](Dura.md): The Mother of Death - life, death, blood
