# The cost model + A11 *force* the whole plot

- **Status:** proposed (observation / consistency note)
- **Target:** cosmology consistency — `The Woven Story.md`, `The Creation Story.md`

## Suggestion
State explicitly, somewhere in the cosmology, that the Allkin's entire arc is *compelled* by two rules acting together:

1. **A11** — you cannot raise (and so cannot mutate) your own Weave.
2. **Heft** — raising a *fresh* Weave is an enormous, usually cooperative cost.

Trapped in an unmutable Aether, the Allkin had exactly one path to ever change their own Weave: **build a foreign Weave (Terrum), imbue it with controllable blood-life, then loop back to mutate the Aether by proxy through them.**

## Why it works
This turns the plot's premise from a *choice* into a *necessity*. The gods didn't create mortals out of whim or cruelty — the laws of the universe left them no other door. That makes the tragedy structural: the cost model doesn't merely permit the story, it *requires* it. Stated plainly, the setup reads as law rather than contrivance.
