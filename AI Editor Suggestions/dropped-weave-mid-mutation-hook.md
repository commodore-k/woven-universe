# Loaded gun: a raised Weave dropped mid-mutation

- **Status:** proposed (story hook)
- **Target:** story / cosmology (consequence of `Weave Theory AI.md` §4)

## Suggestion
§4 establishes that a Weave **drops** if pooled Power falls below an operation's Heft mid-tie. Never yet used: **what a raised, mid-mutation Weave becomes when it falls under load.**

In a cosmos where bonds are *eternal and cannot be undone*, a mutation interrupted at the moment of tying is uniquely horrifying — not undone, not completed, but frozen half-made:
- a half-tied Aspect that half-works, or works wrong;
- an abandoned splice (O6) fused askew;
- an open compound graph (O7) whose Wyrd Function resolved on an incomplete network — an Aspect no-one intended and no-one can name.

## Why it works
It is free grimdark — a direct consequence of a rule you already have, needing no new law. Candidate uses: the origin of a cursed region of Terrum, a malformed or "wrong" Aspect, a wound in the Weave, or a catastrophe in the Allkin's own history (a raise that nearly failed). It also raises the stakes of every future "raising" scene: the reader now knows what dropping costs.
