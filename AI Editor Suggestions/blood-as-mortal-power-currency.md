# Blood is the currency that buys mortal grip

- **Status:** proposed
- **Target:** `Weave Theory AI.md` §6 (BloodMajik) and §7 open-law #2; `Blood Life.md`

## Suggestion
Let the Power/Heft model *define what blood economically is*:

- Mortals have $P \approx 0$ — they can never grip the Strands or raise anything.
- **Blood is the currency that buys enough grip to *wield* an already-established channel** (an anchor pulling a bonded aspect). Majik is blood spent on borrowed grip.
- **Overdraw the grip and you die** — the lethal blood cost. This closes §7 open-law #2 (the blood cost curve): it is finite and lethal to exceed.
- **Weave-Majik (via Dura's Fray) is the lone exception:** a mortal touching *raw* Warp & Weft, so able to do primitive raising-scale things — but only at a trickle, because a Fray is one or two strands of grip.

## Why it works
It unifies three previously separate things — blood, the cost of majik, and mortal death — under the same scalar (grip/Power). Blood stops being a flavor resource and becomes the mortal substitute for the Power the gods have natively. It also cleanly separates the two branches of majik: **BloodMajik** = wielding a channel (cheap grip, pay blood), **Weave-Majik** = raw touch through a flaw (tiny grip, no channel).
