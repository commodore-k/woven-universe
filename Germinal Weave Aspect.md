## Overview
This [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) was woven into the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md) during its [creation](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) by the [Allkin](Allkin.md).

The Allkin [Rastos](Rastos.md) later bonded himself to this Weave Aspect with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).

## Aspect

Imbues the ability for [Sap Life](Sap%20Life.md) to exist in a [Weave](Weave.md) this Aspect is apart of.

## Allkin Weave Cord Use

- Can create/destroy definitions of [Sap Life](Sap%20Life.md) in the Terrum Weave.
- Can create new [Sap Life](Sap%20Life.md) based on the existing definitions in the Terrum Weave.
- Can control movement and growth of [Sap Life](Sap%20Life.md) in the Terrum Weave.

## Terrum [Majik](Majik.md) Use

- Can create new [Sap Life](Sap%20Life.md) based on the existing definitions in the Terrum Weave.
- Can control movement and growth of [Sap Life](Sap%20Life.md) in the Terrum Weave.
