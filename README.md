Terrible Knights is a concept for a video game that is the property of Metal Cactus Studios.

## In One Sentence
> A territory-expansion FPS with RPG and strategy layers, set in a grimdark medieval world with just enough camp to keep it fun

## Elevator Pitch
Knights of the Queendom must help their Queen expand her Realm: deploying out into the world to help establish Villages, build their defenses, endure *terrible nights* fighting off endless hordes of [The Terror](The%20Terror.md), and manage the supply chains to all villages of the Realm to keep them standing day after day. When Knights come back from deployment, they can upgrade their Knight's stats, as well as upgrade and even craft different weapons and gear; additionally they can take on Realm quests from the NPCs as a way to earn some extra coin on the side.
