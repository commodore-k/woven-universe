Terrible Knights is a concept for a video game that is the property of Metal Cactus Studios.

Knights of the Queendom must endure Terrible Nights fighting off The Terror as they help their Queen expand her Realm, deploying out into the world to help Villages build their defenses, fight off the endless horde of The Terror come night, and manage the supply chains to all the other villages in the Realm to keep them standing Night after night.

A mix of Strategy + Extraction Shooter + RPG
