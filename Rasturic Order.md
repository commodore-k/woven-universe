## Overview

This school of Majik is for Maji who anchor themselves to the [Rastur](Rastur.md) [Allkin Bond](Allkin%20Bond.md). Maji of this school can use Majik from any of the following [Weave Aspects](Weave%20Aspect.md):
- [Germinal Weave Aspect](Germinal%20Weave%20Aspect.md)
- [Magnetic Weave Aspect](Magnetic%20Weave%20Aspect.md)
- [Vital Weave Aspect](Vital%20Weave%20Aspect.md)
- [Material Weave Aspect](Material%20Weave%20Aspect.md)
- [Density Weave Aspect](Density%20Weave%20Aspect.md)

Read up on [Weave Anchor](Weave%20Anchor.md)s to understand how Rasturic Artificers craft anchors for Maji.
