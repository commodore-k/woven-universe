
Dura knew humans were entrenched in the lore the Allkin had spun, so, remembering that Rastos had told her about the Creation Story they had spun, she used as little of it as needed to hopefully get their attention so they would listen, but tried to tell as much truth as possible:

---
Hello Verakin, if you hear my voice, I am the the AllKin Dura, I am what you would understand as an All Mother. I hold domain over the Majik of bloodlife, of you, with my Cords. I am thus the only one who can directly create life, death, or take control of you. But my gift to you has been and always will be that I will abstain from ever using these Cords. I make this promise so that you know your life is your own, that you, the bloodlife, have free will.

Now, I wish I could speak more to you, but know this: the blood you give to use the Majik of your All Mothers and All Fathers is causing a sickness in them. I plead you not give into the usage of Majik as it is hurting your Allkin. Know that you are capable and strong beings without it. Take care children.
