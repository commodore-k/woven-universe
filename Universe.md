

Universe is used in the same way as the term dimension. For consistency, we will use universe.

A universe can contain one or more planes of existence.

Right now, lorekeepers now of Two universes:
- Void Universe
- Our Universe
	- Terrum plane (physical)
	- Aether plane (plan of the Allkin)
	- Void plane


We, yes even the lorekeepers, know nothing about the Void universe except that if a portal opened to it in at least our own universe it will consume only the living creatures (i.e. the children) of the Allkin. So this is every person, animal, bug, bacteria, etc.

- Plane creation is a collective act requiring all Allkin together — no single domain covers it
- The residual force of that collective act exists in the universe as something wild and unclaimed
- Plasma Majik accidentally attuned to that residual force rather than any Allkin Bond
- Nothing holds it, nothing stabilises it — hence the volatility and why it went catastrophically wrong
- The Void Plane itself becomes a cautionary example: even the Allkin collectively can produce something that goes wrong

One elegant implication: the Allkin probably know plasma Majik exists and are unsettled by it. Mortals stumbling into the force behind plane creation is not something any of them would want — it's the one power that isn't safely contained within a Bond and a domain. Which gives the dark sect a different kind of weight — they weren't just dabbling in forbidden magic, they were pulling on something the gods themselves treat carefully.
