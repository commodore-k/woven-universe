## Overview
This [Weave Aspect](Weave%20Aspect.md) was woven into the [Terrum Weave](Terrum.md) during its [creation](The%20Woven%20Story.md#The%20Weaving%20of%20[Terrum](Terrum.md)) by the [Allkin](Allkin.md).

The Allkin [Arda](Arda.md) later bonded herself to this Weave Aspect with a [Weave Cord](Weave%20Cords.md).

## Aspect

Imbues the ability for Thermal properties to exist in the Weave this Aspect is apart of.

## Allkin Weave Cord Uses

- Can create/update/destroy thermal definitions/conditions in the Terrum Weave
- Can instill a thermal property on matter or in space based on current thermal definitions in the Terrum Weave

## Terrum [Majik](Majik.md) Uses

- Can instill a thermal property on matter or in space based on current thermal definitions in the Terrum Weave
- Can absorb Thermal conditions when in contact with said thermal condition
	- e.g. can make warm room one is standing in feel cooler since they are touching the warm air
