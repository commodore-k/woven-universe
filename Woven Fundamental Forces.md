The universe is made of a Universal Fiber. This fiber makes up the 3 Strands of the Universe: Warp, Weft, and Wyrd.

## Weave
The Warp and Weft are used to create Weaves, which are planes of existence that are blank by default.

A being made OF a Weave can interact with the Warp and the Weft of their own weave but they cannot interact with the interlacement points of their own weave.

## Wyrd Cord
A Wyrd Cord can be created by braiding all 3 forces together

## Warp Knots
A warp knot can only be created once in the universe and can only be made around the Weft of a Weave.

## Weft Knots
A weft knot can be created as many times within the universe, but it can only be made once on a given Weave. It can only be made around the Warp of a Weave.

## Wyrd Knots
A Wyrd Knot can be created as many times within the universe. Only restriction is that a being within a universe cannot create a Wyrd knot on an interlacement of their own weave. That is to say, it is impossible for them to imbue their own Weave with Wyrd Aspects using Wyrd Knots.

### Affects
- around the warp of a weave
	- nothing happens
- around the weft of a weave
	- nothing happens
- **around an interlacement point of the warp *and* weft in a weave**
	- **imbues a Wyrd Aspect in the Weave**
- around a weft knot
	- pairs said wyrd knot to this weft knot if and only if a matching wyrd knot is made on an interlacement of the same Weave
- around warp knot
	- allows access to control of its Wyrd Aspect in a Weave "at-a-distance" if and only if there is a matching wyrd knot on a weft knot that matches the warp knot for the given Weave. (the matching wyrd knot must of course also be on an interlacement, else there is not Wyrd Aspect to control!)

## Wyrd Aspect
When a specific/unique Wyrd knot is created around an interlacement point of the Warp and Weft in a Weave, it imbues a specific ability / characteristic into the Weave. (e.g. Gravity, time, light, etc.)

You can add the same Wyrd Knot to a given Weave as many times as you want, it has no additional affect on the Weave after the first is made. Adding multiple instances of the same Wyrd Knot type is only useful if you are making a Bended Aspect that wants to blend more than 2 Wyrd Aspects together.

## Bended Knots

Bended Knots create Composite Wyrd Aspects.

To create a Composite Wyrd Aspect, create a Bended Knot on a Weave from two other Wyrd Knots on the same weave, one simply needs to connect one end of the two different Wyrd knots and connect them with a new type of knot that doesn't yet exist on the Weave or for any other Bended knot on the Weave.

Because Wyrd knots can be created more than once on a Weave without additional affects, this can be leveraged if the desired Composite Wyrd Aspect requires a given Wyrd knot to be bended with 3 or more other knots. This is because every knot has at least 2 dangling ends so those two ends can be bended with the ends of two different knots.

As long as the same Bend knot is used to bend two other wyrd strands, then the Bended Knot will represent a new Composite Wyrd Aspect (but the one it previously was imbuing the Weave with will no longer be imbued in the Weave).

Any duplicate bend knots of two weird knots have no additional affect, for example:
Weave: A, B
bended knots: [(A,B), (A,B)]
Wyrd Aspects:
- A: from Knot A
- B: from Knot B
- C: [(A,B)]

## Wyrd Tether

A Wyrd Tether allows a being from one Weave to tether themselves to a Wyrd Knot in another Weave and control that Wyrd Aspect without having to use the Wyrd Knot on that Weave directly. A Tether is a 1-1 connection, once someone is tethered to a Wyrd Knot/Aspect, the tether cannot be undone, and no other being from their Weave or another can be tethered to the same Wyrd Knot, thus only at most 1 being in the universe can control A Wyrd Aspect from a far thanks to a Wyrd Tether.

## Wyrd Bonds
A Wyrd Bond allows beings to combine their Wyrd Tethers. This is done simply by creating a simple loop with just the Wyrd force around an intersection on a Weave. They then tie Wyrd Knots to the loop corresponding to their Wyrd identity Knot. Once a Wyrd Identity Knot is bonded, it cannot be bonded again.

When a Wyrd Bond is made, Anyone tethered to a Wyrd Knot/Aspect that is in that bond can access the other Wyrd Aspects in that Bond. This makes beings in that bond more powerful, this is also the only way to share power. But based on how it works, it obviously requires all parties in the bond to contribute at least one Wyrd Knot/Aspect that they are tethered to.
