A mysterious, advanced technique with [Weave Cords](Weave%20Cords.md) that can join two or more Weave Cords. To splice two or more Weave Cords together means the beings bound to the Weave Cords must undo parts of their Weave Cords and knit connections between their cords, thus entwining them together. This process can also make Composite Weave Aspects if desired, to do so, when splicing the Weave Cords together, the splicing itself needs to also be interwoven into the [Weave](Weave.md). For example, the following  Composite Weave Aspects are required for [Blood Life](Blood%20Life.md) to exist in a [Weave](Weave.md):
- Life Composite Weave Aspect
- Death Composite Weave Aspect
- Blood Composite Weave Aspect
