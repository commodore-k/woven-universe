The Kordona Bloodline is hard to trace back, they are a family that originated from a population of subjects of the Kinnish Empire, and very little on subjects of an Empire were documented in those days. So it is oftened summed up that the Kordona bloodline starts with the establishment they made of the [Emberal Empire](Emberal%20Empire.md) in the [Third Era](Third%20Era.md) - 2431.

It is currently a 625 year old Empire.
